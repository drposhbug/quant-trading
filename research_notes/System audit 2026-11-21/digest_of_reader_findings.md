### Statistics and power of the whole programme (stat_power)
The programme cannot currently return a true PASS and is likely to return a false one. At each sleeve's minimum n, the edge needed for 80% power is 4-7 times the repo's own estimate for favourites (+3.5% to +5.4% a bet against +0.8%), +26-29% ROI for Elo (backtest -6.5%) and +0.14-0.15 of max loss for options (best case -0.01). With zero edge everywhere the seven ROI sleeves still give a 28-56% chance of at least one PASS. The stock sleeve's 3% tracking-error bar fails by construction: the 15:30-versus-close clock offset alone is 5.3% a year. Twelve months cannot measure skill against any benchmark (12-80 years needed). The repo holds about 1,150 tests in three days; the prior for the next predictive idea is about 2%, where the current promotion rule is wrong 62-75% of the time. Retrospective favourites numbers shrink almost entirely (+11% to about 0; +0.8% to -0.15%). Recommend no new predictive test: fix the yardstick, pool favourites into one judged-once test, require P <= 0.005 or two independent passes with a four-idea budget, stop the Elo, IPO and NFL/NHL/MLB sleeves, and keep the Alpaca account.
On the reset: Keep the account as it is. Two sessions carry no information about skill and a reset changes no future return; restarting the record after a bad start is selective reporting, and it would move the pre-committed -10% review tier from 5 points away to 10.
Ideas: Measure tracking error on end-of-day equity (p=0.9); One pooled favourites test, clustered and judged once (p=0.03); Promotion bar of P <= 0.005 or two independent passes, with a four-idea budget (p=0.8); Holdout clause: judge the gold sleeve only on untouched 2004-2015 data (p=0.15)
