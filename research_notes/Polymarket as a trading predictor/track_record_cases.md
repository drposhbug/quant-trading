# Polymarket and Kalshi track record for traders, 2020 to Nov 2026: dated case studies

Scope note: compiled 2026-11-19 from about 18 searches and fetches. Much of the coverage is crypto-friendly or aggregator press. "Documented timing" means a source gives timestamps for both the odds and the asset move. Everything else is labeled co-movement or anecdote. Hindsight bias is a big risk here: "Polymarket called it" stories are written after the fact and pick winners.

## 1. 2024 US presidential election: odds vs polls, and odds vs DJT, crypto, yields and the peso

### Takeaway
In October 2024, Polymarket moved toward Trump while polls and 538 still showed a toss-up. DJT, bitcoin, the dollar and 10y yields rallied in the same month, but no source shows the odds moving before the assets. The best-documented lead is election night: Polymarket passed 90-95% about 6 hours before the AP call. That is a lead over the media, not over futures, which were trading at the same time.

### Cited Findings
- On Oct 19, 2024, 538 had Trump at 52/100, a "dead heat". Month to date: DJT +83%, bitcoin +12%, the dollar index about +2%, the 10y yield about +40bp. The article gives no day-by-day order of odds, polls and assets, and suggests the Polymarket shift "may have come from a few large bettors" — [Fortune, Oct 19 2024](https://fortune.com/2024/10/19/who-will-win-the-presidential-election-poll-trump-media-stock-bitcoin-dollar/)
- Polymarket: Trump 57.9% vs Harris 41.8% on Oct 15, 2024, and a 16-point lead on Oct 16 — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2024-10-16-trump-leads-16-over-harris-polymarket-321336)
- Near the end of October, Trump odds fell from 67% to 61% in 48 hours. Crypto slumped alongside. DJT fell 34% in 3 days after gaining 352% over the previous month — [CoinDesk, Oct 31 2024](https://irishnews.pages.dev/crypto-news-coindesk.com/nl/markets/2024/10/31/crypto-slumps-alongside-trumps-victory-odds-on-polymarket-as-uncertainty-and-profit-taking-rise) (mirror of a CoinDesk article)
- Strategists named the "Trump trade" in the dollar, especially against the Mexican peso, and in bitcoin — [Reuters via Investing.com](https://investing.com/news/economy-news/analysisfrom-bitcoin-to-mexican-peso-trump-trades-are-appearing-again-3670102)
- Whether yields were driven by the election odds is disputed. Bloomberg opinion and ZeroHedge argued Polymarket was setting near-term Treasury and FX signals. Tom Essaye (Sevens Report) said it made little sense to blame the October yield run-up mostly on Trump fiscal fears — [Bloomberg/Authers opinion, Oct 25 2024](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2024-10-25/prediction-markets-reflect-that-the-election-clock-favors-trump); [ZeroHedge mirror](https://solwd.net/polymarket-is-singlehandedly-moving-the-entire-us-bond-market/); [Reuters via WHBL](https://whbl.com/?p=742420)
- Going into Election Day: Polymarket Trump 62%, Kalshi 57%. On election night, the aggregate passed 90% by 11:00pm ET, Polymarket hit about 95% at 11:43pm ET, and the AP called it at 5:34am ET. Most of this timeline comes from advocacy pieces — [Haseeb Qureshi (Dragonfly)](https://haseebq.com/what-polymarket-got-right-that-the-experts-got-wrong/); [The Block, Nov 6 2024](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2024-11-06-donald-trump-polymarket-324738); [The Block resolution](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2024-11-06-polymarket-resolves-us-presidential-election-winner-bets-324563)
- A French whale ("Théo") made about $40M+ on Trump bets. This is the source of the "few large bettors" concern about October prices — [Fortune, Nov 6 2024](https://fortune.com/2024/11/06/polymarket-whale-won-40-million-betting-on-trump/)
- Polymarket's popular-vote market favored Harris, and Trump won the popular vote. So one of the headline markets was wrong — [Fortune, Oct 18 2024](https://fortune.com/2024/10/18/presidential-election-odds-pro-trump-bettors-polymarket-political-polls-harris/)

### Inferences
- The October 2024 evidence is co-movement within the month, not a lead. DJT, BTC and the peso all traded the same news flow as Polymarket, and all were open 24/7 or nearly so (except DJT).
- On election night, S&P futures, the peso and the dollar traded live alongside Polymarket. The "6 hours before AP" lead is over the media call, not a tradable lead over futures. No source here shows Polymarket leading futures by minutes.
- Treat "Polymarket beat the polls" as one draw. Kalshi at 57-59% was only mildly confident, and the popular-vote market was wrong.

### Gaps
- No intraday, timestamped study of Polymarket vs ES futures, MXN or 10y on Nov 5-6, 2024 was found. Answering this needs Polymarket CLOB history plus intraday futures data.
- No sourced daily-level Granger or lead-lag test for October 2024 was found.

## 2. Fed decisions 2023-2026: Kalshi and Polymarket vs fed funds futures, including FEDS 2026-010

### Takeaway
The Fed's own FEDS 2026-010 (Diercks, Katz and Wright, Feb 2026) finds the Kalshi mode matched the realized FOMC decision on the day before every meeting since 2022, and a statistically significant improvement over Bloomberg consensus for headline CPI. This is accuracy on near-certain events at the 1-day horizon. It is not evidence that Kalshi moves before futures intraday.

### Cited Findings
- FEDS 2026-010, "Kalshi and the Rise of Macro Markets", Feb 2026. The abstract calls Kalshi "a high-frequency, continuously updated, distributionally rich benchmark" and says the paper studies how expectations respond to macro and financial news — [Federal Reserve](https://federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/kalshi-and-the-rise-of-macro-markets.htm); [PDF](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/files/2026010pap.pdf)
- Quoted from the paper: "The mode of the Kalshi distribution ... has perfectly matched the realized federal funds rate by the day of each meeting since 2022, a feat not achieved by either surveys or futures." The paper reports a perfect record for the Kalshi median and mode on the day before FOMC, a statistically significant improvement over fed funds futures — [Fortune summary](https://fortune.com/2026/01/28/kalshi-prediction-market-federal-reserve-betting-forecast-nber-working-paper/); [DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/news/federal-reserve-study-finds-kalshi-markets-rival-traditional-economic-forecast-tools/); [NatLawReview](https://natlawreview.com/article/federal-reserve-researchers-find-prediction-markets-deliver-forecasting-value)
- A secondary summary gives a fed funds futures MAE of 0.010 vs 0.000 for the Kalshi median and mode. Unverified: the PDF text could not be extracted here — [Libertify summary](https://www.libertify.com/interactive-library/kalshi-macro-prediction-markets-federal-reserve-feds-2026-010/)
- The paper finds a statistically significant improvement over the Bloomberg consensus for headline CPI — [Fortune, Aug 13 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/prediction-markets-reputation-surprise-result-wisconsin/) (quoting the Fed paper)
- A related NBER working paper (Jan 2026, not peer reviewed) finds Kalshi about as accurate as Wall Street forecasts — [Fortune, Jan 28 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/01/28/kalshi-prediction-market-federal-reserve-betting-forecast-nber-working-paper/)
- In this repo's own Nov 18, 2026 snapshot, the Polymarket Dec 2026 FOMC market had a 25bp hike at 91.5% and no change at 2.15% — local file `signals/prediction_markets.csv` (row ts 2026-11-18T21:44:49Z)

### Inferences
- A 0.000 vs 0.010 MAE the day before FOMC is mostly a quantization effect. Futures price a continuous expected rate that includes term premium and month-averaging, while the Kalshi mode is a discrete bucket. The comparison favors Kalshi by construction, so it is not evidence that Kalshi knows something futures don't.
- For trading, the relevant question is whether Kalshi reprices before SOFR or fed funds futures after a data release or Fed speak. The paper's news-response section may cover this, but it could not be extracted.

### Gaps
- The FEDS 2026-010 PDF body could not be read (binary extraction failed). Its high-frequency news-response results, sample size and meeting count are unverified here.
- No minute-level lead-lag study of Kalshi FOMC markets vs futures was found.

## 3. Tariffs, government shutdowns and CPI, 2025-2026

### Takeaway
Tariff and recession markets reacted to the April 2025 tariff shock at the same time as equities, then partly reversed. Recession odds peaked at 66-71% and no 2025 recession followed. That is a costly overreaction, not a lead. The Oct-Nov 2025 shutdown end-date markets were too pessimistic. The CPI evidence is mixed: one case of pre-release drift, and the Fed paper's CPI accuracy result.

### Cited Findings
- After Liberation Day (Apr 2, 2025), Polymarket's 2025 recession odds went from 46% to 54% in 24 hours, with Kalshi at 54%. The Dow fell about 1,300 points — [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/25/04/44640419/recession-in-2025-crypto-betters-now-see-over-50-probability-after-trumps-sweeping-tariff-); [Sherwood](https://www.sherwood.news/markets/odds-of-a-2025-recession-soar-over-50-on-prediction-markets-kalshi-polymarket/)
- Recession odds then fell: Polymarket from a 66% peak in early April to 39%, Kalshi from 71% to 38%, after US-China de-escalation in May 2025 — [Benzinga, May 2025](https://drupal-cloudfront.benzinga.com/25/05/45535042/recession-odds-tumble-on-kalshi-crypto-based-polymarket-as-trumps-tariff-concerns-subside)
- Before the April 9, 2025 pause, Polymarket bettors priced tariffs staying in place through April, and Business Insider reported "small odds Trump backs down". The 90-day pause on Apr 9 contradicted them — [Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/prediction-markets-stir-as-trumps-trade-war-strategy-evolves/); [Business Insider NL](https://www.businessinsider.nl/betting-markets-see-small-odds-trump-backs-down-from-tariffs-in-the-coming-months/)
- Shutdown 2025: it began Oct 1, 2025. In mid-October, traders priced 44% that it lasted to Nov 15 and about one in three past Nov 20. It lasted 43 days, ending about Nov 12 — [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/prediction-markets-signal-government-shutdown-may-last-mid-november); [iHeart, Oct 20 2025](https://wtam.iheart.com/content/2025-10-20-prediction-markets-betting-that-government-shutdown-wont-end-anytime-soon); [PredictionHunt](https://www.predictionhunt.com/odds/2025-government-shutdown)
- CPI: per a Keyrock report (Dec 16, 2025), the Kalshi "Inflation in 2025" implied forecast rose from about 3.05% to 3.58% before the April 10 CPI release, while the Cleveland Fed nowcast stayed near 3.9%. The report gives no outcome scoring and no asset-price follow-through — [DL News](https://dlnews.com/articles/web3/prediction-markets-act-as-leading-indicators-for-inflation-data/)
- This repo's own test (pm_study.py, Nov 18, 2026): 4 of 113 predictive tests passed Holm, and 0 survived dropping the 5 largest odds moves. 5 of 59 same-day tests were significant. The SHUTDOWN series "passes" for TSM and ORCL rest on 3 dates (2026-01-28 +59pp; 2024-02-28; 2023-09-20). Conclusion: odds co-move with stocks on the same day far more reliably than they lead — local file `reports/pm_results.md`

### Inferences
- In policy-shock episodes, prediction markets and equities react to the same headline at the same time. Odds-based "recession" or "tariff" signals in April 2025 would have sold near the low.
- Policy-event markets driven by one decision-maker (pause, deal, shutdown end) show no edge in these cases. Liberation Day and the shutdown end date were both mispriced.

### Gaps
- No timestamped evidence was found of a tariff-deal market (e.g. US-China Geneva on May 12, 2025, or the UK deal) moving before the equity tape.
- The year of the "April 10 CPI" in the Keyrock example is not stated (probably 2025), and there is no accuracy follow-up.
- No source was found on how the S&P moved relative to shutdown-odds swings.

## 4. IPO markets: CoreWeave, Circle, Figma, SpaceX and Anthropic

### Takeaway
Before 2026 there is little documented prediction-market pricing for IPO opens or closes. Kalshi's IPO markets in Aug 2025 were "will X IPO by date" markets, not open or close markets. The 2026 mega-IPOs had close and market-cap markets. SpaceX resolved correctly at a 98% favorite with lots of room to spare. For Anthropic, the market was wildly overconfident a month out (about 90% for a close above $1.5T) but got the right close bracket on the day ($1.25-1.5T, about 62%). The sample is too small to call a systematic bias, but in both 2026 cases the markets were more cautious than the open on the day itself.

### Cited Findings
- CoreWeave (Mar 2025), Circle (Jun 2025) and Figma (Jul 2025): no Polymarket or Kalshi open, close or market-cap market pricing was found. Kalshi's Aug 1, 2025 post lists only IPO-timing markets (SpaceX 20% public by mid-2027, Klarna 74% announces IPO by year end, OpenAI 17% before June 2027, etc.) and cites Figma's debut only as context — [Kalshi News](https://news.kalshi.com/p/kalshis-new-ipo-markets)
  - Figma, Jul 31, 2025: priced at $33, opened at $85 (+158%), closed at $115.50 (+250%), about $46-47B market cap. Before the open, market-maker indications reported by Bloomberg were $105-110, versus the $85 open. That is a broker indication, not a prediction market — [IPOScoop](https://www.iposcoop.com/the-ipo-buzz-figma-fig-opens-at-85-up-158-percent-tops-100-in-nyse-debut/); [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2025/07/31/figma-ipo-stock-pop-triple-valuation-dylan-field); [Capital Brief](https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/figma-shares-set-to-debut-at-over-triple-ipo-price-12d4297a-69a9-49b4-a508-57676925caac/)
  - In Business Insider's reporting, CoreWeave's and Figma's debuts lifted odds for later IPOs (Cerebras, Databricks) — [AOL/BI](https://www.aol.com/figmas-ipo-huge-hit-companies-175844972.html)
- SpaceX (SPCX), June 2026: a Kalshi first-day-close contract with about $3M volume priced a 98% chance of closing above a $1.2T market cap. SPCX priced at $135, closed day one at $160.95 (+19%), about $2.1T (another source uses a different share count and gets about $1.75T). Either way the contract resolved YES. Shares were down about 50% from the post-IPO-week peak by late July 2026 — [The Information (paywalled)](https://feature.theinformation.com/newsletters/the-information-finance/pre-ipo-betting-boom-hits-prediction-markets); [CNBC, Jun 15 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/15/spacex-stock-record-ipo-debut.html); [MoneyWeek](https://moneyweek.com/investments/tech-stocks/spacex-ipo); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_public_offering_of_SpaceX)
- Polymarket "largest IPO by market cap in 2026": SpaceX was about 65% earlier in 2026. This repo's snapshot on Nov 19, 2026 21:41Z shows 99.35% — [CoinStats/Polymarket](https://coinstats.app/prediction-markets/odds/economy/largest-ipo-by-market-cap-in-2026-287/); local `signals/prediction_markets.csv`
- Anthropic (ANT), Nov 19, 2026: the range was $70-78 and it priced at $83, raising about $81B, for a basic market cap of about $1.1T at $83. Polymarket first-day close odds per Yahoo (published Nov 19, 11:47am ET; the snapshot time is not given):
  - over $1.5T: 27.95%, down from 89.6% a month earlier
  - $1.25-1.5T: 61.95%, up from 8% a month earlier and 39.85% the prior day
  - $1.5-1.75T: 25.7%
  - under $1.25T: 8.65%
  - over $2T: 2.05%
  - about $5.81M in 24h volume
  - clearing $1.5T needed about +36%, roughly $113/share

  — [Yahoo Finance, Nov 19 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-1-5-trillion-ipo-164738953.html)
- Anthropic outcome (per the user's tracking; a Yahoo "fades from $111.50 open" headline corroborates the open, but no independent source for the close was found): priced $83, opened $111.50 (+34%), closed $97.17 (+17%). The closing market cap was about 13.3B basic shares × $97.17 ≈ $1.29T. The share count is derived from the article's $1.1T at $83, not reported. That close falls inside the $1.25-1.5T bracket the market favored at about 62%. At the open, $111.50 × 13.3B ≈ $1.48T, just under the $1.5T line.
- Earlier Anthropic timing markets: Kalshi and Polymarket diverged on Anthropic and OpenAI IPO timing. Polymarket priced 93.1% for a first trade by Nov 19 (as of Nov 13). Kalshi pays on IPO confirmation while Polymarket pays on first trade. Kalshi odds fell 20 cents in 29 hours at one point — [Totalis](https://www.totalis.trade/blogs/prediction-markets/anthropic-ipo-odds); [Blockchain.News](https://blockchain.news/news/kalshi-polymarket-anthropic-openai-ipo-odds); [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-anthropic-ipo-odds-fell-20-cents-in-29-hours-on-kalshi/); [CNBC, Oct 26 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/prediction-markets-think-anthropic-ipo-near-certain-in-2026.html)

### Inferences
- Was there a systematic bias? With n ≈ 2 market-cap markets, no claim is possible. The pattern is:
  - Anthropic was badly overestimated a month out (90% for over $1.5T) and correct on the day. The odds converged only after pricing ($83) became known, so the market followed the book-build rather than leading it.
  - SpaceX's 98% contract had a low strike that was easy to clear.
- On Anthropic's open-vs-close shape: the open ($111.50, about $1.48T) was near the over-$1.5T line, and the stock faded to $97.17. A trader reading the 28% over-$1.5T price before the open would have expected the close to stay below $1.5T, which was correct. The open's +34% pop was larger than the close bracket implied.
- Hindsight caution: the Yahoo snapshot may postdate the open. The article was published at 11:47am ET, and large Nasdaq IPOs often open around midday. If the snapshot came after the open, it is not a pre-open forecast.
- In the Figma case, the broker indications before the open ($105-110) overshot the open ($85), but the close ($115.50) exceeded the indications. That is one example of an "open below indication, close above" path, the opposite shape to Anthropic.

### Gaps
- No archived Polymarket or Kalshi open-price, close-price or market-cap markets for CoreWeave, Circle or Figma were found. Whether such markets existed in 2025 is unconfirmed.
- No pre-open (pre-9:30am or pre-first-trade) timestamped Anthropic odds were found, and there is no Kalshi Anthropic close-bracket data. This repo did not snapshot Anthropic markets before the open (commit 1b4d08d is dated after the close, 23:40 ET on Nov 19).
- The SPCX day-one market cap differs by share count ($1.75T vs $2.1T). The exact Kalshi resolution source was not found.

## 5. Notable failures and resolution disputes

### Takeaway
Confident-but-wrong cases cluster in thin, single-decision or low-information markets: primaries, foreign elections, policy pivots. Resolution risk is real, especially at Polymarket via UMA's token-weighted votes, where a contract can resolve against the plain-language reading.

### Cited Findings
- Zelensky suit market (Jun-Jul 2025, about $160-237M volume): it first resolved YES on Jul 1, was disputed, and was resolved NO by UMA, despite widespread reporting that he wore a suit. Fewer than 10 UMA holders supplied about 18M staked UMA, and 3 addresses supplied more than 70% of the vote weight. UMA's Hart Lambur denied manipulation — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/329210/polymarket-rules-no-237m-controversial-bet-over-zelenskyy-suit?amp=1); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/nfts-and-web3/polymarket-controversy-heats-up-after-the-zelenskyy-suit-market-resolves); [Protos](https://protos.com/zelenskyy-suit-polymarket-dispute)
- 2024 Venezuelan election: a UMA governance fight led Polymarket to override its own rules — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/polymarket-caught-in-crossfire-over-zelenskyy-suit-bet)
- 2025 NYC mayoral primary: Kalshi and Polymarket favored Cuomo, and Mamdani won. A poll was more accurate — [Next Event Horizon](https://nexteventhorizon.substack.com/p/kalshi-moved-the-goalposts-on-nyc-mayor)
- 2026 Wisconsin Democratic governor primary: markets favored Hong and Crowley won. Fortune called it a dent in the markets' "near-infallible reputation" — [Fortune, Aug 13 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/prediction-markets-reputation-surprise-result-wisconsin/); [DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/?p=17770)
- 2026 Brazil presidential election: Polymarket had Flávio Bolsonaro as the overwhelming favorite "right up until" he lost — [Futurism](https://futurism.com/future-society/prediction-markets-wrong-brazilian-election)
- 2024 popular vote: Polymarket favored Harris — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/10/18/presidential-election-odds-pro-trump-bettors-polymarket-political-polls-harris/)
- April 2025 recession odds (66-71%) and the "Trump won't back down" pricing before the Apr 9 pause (see section 3)
- Systematic bias: a 2026 CESifo study of more than 300,000 Kalshi contracts found a favorite-longshot bias, with low-priced contracts winning less often than their prices implied. A Vanderbilt study reported "correct" rates of Polymarket 67%, Kalshi 78% and PredictIt 93%. That methodology is crude, and the date and sample are unknown — [Fortune, Aug 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/prediction-markets-reputation-surprise-result-wisconsin/); [DL News](https://dlnews.com/articles/web3/prediction-markets-act-as-leading-indicators-for-inflation-data/)
- Volume caveat: Paradigm researchers argue Polymarket volume has been double-counted — [DL News](https://dlnews.com/articles/web3/prediction-markets-act-as-leading-indicators-for-inflation-data/)

### Inferences
- For traders, the main risks are: (a) thin markets swung by single whales (Oct 2024 Trump odds; Anthropic Kalshi -20c in 29h), (b) resolution and oracle risk (UMA), and (c) favorite-longshot bias, which makes cheap tails look more likely than they are.
- Across these cases, prediction markets did best on scheduled, data-anchored events (FOMC the day before, election-night counting) and worst on discretionary political decisions and thinly traded primaries.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed lead-lag study (odds → asset returns, intraday) covering 2020-2026 was found. This repo's own pm_study.py (Nov 2026) is the most direct test available, and it finds no robust lead.
- Pre-2024 cases (2020 election, 2022 midterms on Polymarket or PredictIt) were not researched, given the tool-call budget.
