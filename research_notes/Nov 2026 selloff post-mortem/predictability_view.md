# Predictability / flows-and-positioning view: could a rules-based retail trader have seen Nov 2026 coming?

Research date 2026-11-21. Episode facts are from the brief. Evidence is published out-of-sample work, not this episode. "Unverified" means I saw it only in a search snippet or secondary summary, or could not open the primary source.

## Short answer

No, not in a tradable sense. Fragility was visible for a year. Timing was not. Every gauge that "called" November had also fired repeatedly before new highs. The two tools with real evidence behind them (trend gate, vol targeting) are already in the frozen model, and they did their job: -5.0% against QQQ -7.7% over two sessions.

## Signal by signal

| # | Signal | Best evidence | Daily, long-only tradable? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BofA FMS cash ≤4% | Sell-side only; about -2.4% at 1 month, -0.7% at 3 months (global equities) | No: monthly, proprietary | Fragility gauge only |
| 2 | Low implied correlation | Academic: low IC predicts *lower* returns up to 1 year | Data free (COR3M); horizon is months | Fragility gauge only |
| 3 | AAII bears >50% | AAII's own study: "mixed record" | Weekly; fails in bear markets | No |
| 4 | 200-day break, CTA selling | Trend gate cuts drawdown; no measured flow impact | Yes; already in model | Useful as risk control; front-running no |
| 5 | CAPE, concentration | R² about 4% at 1 year, about 26-43% at 10 years | No horizon match | No (for timing) |
| 6 | Tightening regime, no Fed put | Fed put documented; its absence is not tested as a timing rule | Yes (yields are free) | Fragility gauge; one test candidate |
| 7 | Crash models | No independent false-alarm rate; already 0/44 in this repo | Mostly yes | No |
| 8 | Single-name event drift | Drift after investigations exists but in small/mid-cap samples | Needs an event feed | No |

**1. Fund-manager cash.** BofA reports 12 prior sell signals since 2011, with global equities -2.4% over the next month and -0.7% over three months ([Bloomberg, Dec 2024](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-12-17/bofa-s-sell-signal-for-stocks-triggered-as-cash-holdings-tumble); [Investing.com](https://investing.com/news/stock-market-news/bofa-says-stock-market-is-flashing-sell-signal-3776584)). A later report cites a median decline of only -1% over 24 signals in 15 years (unverified, snippet). The rule is the vendor's own backtest, the threshold has no independent replication, and I found no hit-rate table. It also fired in Dec 2024, Jul 2025, Nov 2025 (3.7%), Dec 2025 (3.3%, record low), May 2026 and Jul 2026 ([Bloomberg, Nov 2025](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-11-18/stocks-face-sell-signal-as-cash-holdings-decline-in-bofa-survey); [Morningstar/MarketWatch, Dec 2025](https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/2025121634/fund-managers-hold-record-low-in-cash-as-sentiment-hits-four-year-high); [investingLive, May 2026](https://investinglive.com/stock-market-update/us-stocks-boa-warns-of-summer-stock-rout-as-cash-levels-fall-and-bullish-sentiment-peaks-20260527/)). The S&P 500 made a record on 2026-10-06. A signal that is on most of the time cannot time anything. The 11-17 reading also came after the first leg down.

**2. Implied correlation.** The academic sign is that higher implied correlation predicts higher market returns for up to a year, so a low reading points to weaker returns ([AEA 2018 paper](https://aeaweb.org/conference/2018/preliminary/paper/Rt6FbZde); [Driessen et al., Netspar](https://www.netspar.nl/wp-content/uploads/061_Driessen.pdf), R² 12-15% jointly with the variance risk premium). That agrees with "no contagion priced" as a warning. The horizon is quarters, the samples start around 1996, and I found no study of the Cboe COR3M index or of a "low correlation plus high VIX" rule. Low correlation was also the normal state of the 2024-26 megacap market.

**3. AAII bears.** AAII's own study (data to 2013): after bearish readings 2σ above average the S&P 500 rose over the next 6 months 60% of the time, against a 71% unconditional base rate. The mean gain was 2.8%. Excluding Nov 2007-Feb 2009 the mean jumps to 14.2% ([AAII Journal](https://www.aaii.com/journal/article/is-the-aaii-sentiment-survey-a-contrarian-indicator)). So the signal is worse than a coin weighted by the base rate, and all of its failures sit in one regime: early in a real bear market. A related sentiment signal failed early in 1974, 2002, 2008 and 2022 ([SentimenTrader, 2025](https://sentimentrader.com/blog/surveys-say-investors-gave-upthen-hurried-back)). Three of those four were tightening or no-put regimes. The "16% median 12-month return" figures in the press ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2025/04/25/wall-street-dubious-history-near-100-success-rate)) lean on 2009 and overlapping weekly readings.

**4. 200-day break and systematic selling.** Trend gates work as risk control. Faber reports the S&P 500 at about 14% annualised with 14% vol above its 10-month average and about 3% with 24% vol below it, 1901-2008 (secondary summary, unverified against the paper's tables; [SSRN 962461 copy](https://www.scribd.com/doc/116881446/SSRN-id962461)). Volatility targeting reliably cuts left tails in equities ([Harvey et al., JPM 2018](https://people.duke.edu/~charvey/Research/Published_Papers/P135_The_impact_of.pdf)), though it does not reliably raise Sharpe out of sample ([Cederburg et al., JFE 2020](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:138:y:2020:i:1:p:95-117)). The cost is whipsaw: the S&P crossed its 200-day about 180 times in 1994-2019 ([StockCharts](https://stockcharts.com/articles/arthurhill/2019/02/just-how-effective-is-the-200-day-moving-average.html)). Front-running the flow is a different claim and has no support. The ECB could not size the March 2020 effect ([ECB FSR 2020](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/financial-stability/fsr/focus/2020/html/ecb.fsrbox202005_02~f6616db9be.en.html)). Short-term equity trend has decayed because faster players already trade ahead of it ([Kurth et al., arXiv 2607.01550](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.01550)). A daily, close-only account is the party being front-run. QQQ first closed below the 200-day on 11-13 and the worst day came five sessions later, so the gate helped here. This repo's H4 band test already failed.

**5. Valuation and concentration.** CAPE explains about 4% of 1-year real returns, 26% at 10 years (unverified blog figure) and 43% at 10 years in Vanguard's 1926-2011 sample (secondary). Estrada (2026) finds valuation multiples "not helpful" for dynamic allocation ([IESE](https://blog.iese.edu/jestrada/files/2026/05/M4V.pdf)). Goyal, Welch and Zafirov re-tested 46 predictors: over a third fail in sample and half of the rest fail out of sample ([RFS 2024](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/rfinst/v37y2024i11p3490-3557..html)). Concentration evidence is about 5-year relative returns of the top 10 against the rest ([CFA Institute](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/blogs/enterprising-investor/2025/market-concentration-and-lost-decades)). S&P DJI says high concentration "is not necessarily predictive." The most relevant paper is Greenwood, Shleifer and You: a 100% industry run-up does not predict low average returns, but it does raise crash probability, and the peak comes on average 5.8 months after the run-up is identified ([JFE 2019](https://shleifer.scholars.harvard.edu/publications/bubbles-fama)). My recollection of roughly half of such episodes crashing is unverified; I could not open the PDF. This describes 2026 well: the warning was right and a year early. I found no tested AI capex-to-revenue signal.

**6. Tightening regime.** Since 1994 a 10% equity fall predicts 32 bp of cuts at the next meeting and 127 bp within a year. The response is asymmetric ([Cieslak and Vissing-Jorgensen, RFS 2021](https://www.nber.org/papers/w26894)). Dip-buying statistics from 1994 onward therefore embed a put that is absent when a hike is 91% priced. Corrections during hiking cycles average about 15% and depth tracks recessions more than rates ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/rate-hikes-returns-and-recessions), secondary). I found no rigorous table of drawdown depth by policy regime and no tested timing rule. Note that this repo's H5 re-entry episodes were weakest in 2001, 2002 and 2015.

**7. Crash models.** LPPLS: the only cited false-positive figure (0.2%) is on synthetic GARCH data and is contested; out-of-sample claims come from the method's authors ([Vaasa paper](https://osuva.uwasa.fi/bitstreams/e834bca8-9b22-45b6-906c-136d867ec80c/download)). Absorption ratio and turbulence: "most drawdowns were preceded by spikes" is the wrong conditional; no false-alarm rate is published ([State Street](https://globalmarkets.statestreet.com/research/portal/insights/article/2a8e282a-343b-443c-b362-1634c71d2d26)). VIX backwardation occurs under 20% of days since 2010 and "doesn't pan out" outside the big selloffs ([Cboe](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/inside-volatility-trading-is-vix-backwardation-necessarily-a-sign-of-a-future-down-market)). SKEW overstates tail frequency ([blog test](https://www.r-bloggers.com/2020/01/skew-who/), not peer reviewed). Credit spreads forecast recessions, not equity timing ([Fed note](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econresdata/notes/feds-notes/2016/recession-risk-and-the-excess-bond-premium-20160408.html)). This repo already tested VIX level, term structure and credit: 0 of 44.

**8. Single-name events.** SEC investigation announcements: about -6% mean at announcement ([McDowell 2005](https://archive.nyu.edu/bitstream/2451/25947/2/McDowell_2005.pdf)); disclosing firms lag peers by about 12.7% over a year ([Berkeley](https://lawcat.berkeley.edu:443/record/1129102/files/fulltext.pdf), working paper); enforcement news drifts about -108 bp over a week ([SBFC 2025](https://sbfc.sydney.edu.au/2025/papers/SBFC2025_3E1_P114.pdf), conference draft). Forced CEO exits show no consistent sign ([MDPI](https://mdpi.com/2227-7072/9/3/34/xml)), but volatility stays higher for a long time ([Clayton et al.](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr166.pdf)). These samples are mostly smaller firms. I found nothing on megacaps, and a founder-CEO exit at the largest index weight has no reference class. The inverse-vol weights already cut a name whose volatility jumps.

## What deserves a pre-registered test

My recommendation is **at most one, at the scheduled review, and it is defensible to run none.** About 700 prior tests plus yesterday's 0-of-8 put the base rate under 2%. Any rule suggested by this episode is selected on it, so November 2026 must be excluded from the judged window.

| Candidate | Why | P(pass) |
|---|---|---|
| **Tightening-regime gate.** When QQQ is below its 200-day and the 2-year yield is above its level 126 sessions earlier, gross is 25% of target instead of 50%. Judge on ^NDX 1990-2026-10 and both stock universes, metric MaxDD, Sharpe no worse than -0.05. | Only item with a causal mechanism (the asymmetric put), free data, and no overlap with earlier tests. About five independent regimes, so power is poor. | 5-8% |
| Implied correlation (COR3M bottom decile and VIX above its 1-year median → scale 0.75) | Academic sign agrees; data from 2007 only; horizon mismatch | 3-5% |
| Idiosyncratic-shock cut (name falls 3σ more than the basket → halve that name for 20 sessions) | Drift evidence is weak for megacaps; close to the rejected trend/reversal signal | 3-5% |
| FMS cash, AAII, CAPE, LPPLS, SKEW, absorption ratio | No daily data, no independent evidence, or already failed here | under 2%; do not test |

## Looked for and did not find

- An independent hit-rate table for the BofA cash rule, or S&P 500 (not global) forward returns.
- Any study of COR3M itself, or of low correlation combined with high single-stock vol.
- A complete list of AAII >50% readings with forward returns by regime.
- A peer-reviewed price-impact estimate for CTA or vol-control selling, or any evidence that published trigger levels can be traded ahead of at daily frequency.
- False-alarm rates for LPPLS on real data, the absorption ratio, or turbulence.
- Post-event drift evidence for megacaps, or for CEO exit and regulator probe together.
- Greenwood-Shleifer-You crash counts (PDF unreadable here).
