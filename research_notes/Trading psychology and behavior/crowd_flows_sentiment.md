# Crowd Behavior, Retail Flows and Sentiment in US Equities (2020–2026)

Evidence labels used below: **[ACADEMIC]** peer-reviewed or working paper; **[SELL-SIDE/BROKER DATA]** flow reports from JPM / Citadel / Vanda / Nasdaq / Cboe (proprietary, not independently replicable, reported via press); **[PRACTITIONER BACKTEST]** blogs / vendors (no out-of-sample discipline, often promotional); **[COMMENTARY]** narrative.

## Q1. What do flow data say about retail behavior in mega-cap tech 2023–2026 (incl. 2025 tariff dip-buying and 2026 events)?

### Takeaway
Retail became a persistent, record-setting dip buyer from 2023 through mid-2026, concentrated in NVDA, TSLA, PLTR and their leveraged single-stock ETFs. The behavior is the opposite of the "retail panics" stereotype: in April 2025 and repeatedly in 2026, retail bought hardest on down days, and the 2020–2025 cycle rewarded it. There are early signs of fatigue (JPM, spring 2026). The data come from proprietary broker or market-maker feeds, so totals differ by source.

### Cited Findings
**2025 tariff selloff ("Liberation Day")**
- [SELL-SIDE] JPMorgan: individuals net bought **$4.7bn** of US equities on 3 April 2025, the largest daily inflow in about a decade. The buying was split roughly evenly between single stocks ($2.3bn) and ETFs ($2.4bn). JPM contrasted this with March 2020, when individuals *sold* on down days. Some outlets date the $4.7bn to 4 April. — [itiger/MarketWatch repost](https://itiger.com/news/2525073858); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/05/15/stocks-tariffs-tesla-nvidia-palantir)
- [SELL-SIDE] JPM retail flows: **$36bn net in March 2025 and $40bn in April 2025**, back-to-back monthly records. Retail's share of order flow hit an all-time high of **36% on 29 April 2025**. About $50bn was bought after 8 April (Bloomberg, via secondary sources). — [JPMorgan AM "Who is buying US equities"](https://am.jpmorgan.com/us/en/asset-management/liq/insights/market-insights/market-updates/on-the-minds-of-investors/who-is-buying-us-equities); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/05/15/stocks-tariffs-tesla-nvidia-palantir)
- [SELL-SIDE] During the Liberation Day selloff, investors kept buying leveraged TSLA and NVDA funds even as the stocks fell more than 30% (Direxion / Vanda / The Compound study, reported by Reuters). — [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/etfs/leveraged-etfs-ride-retail-trading-boom-as-single-stock-bets-redefine-market-stress-playbook/265389); [itiger/Reuters](https://www.itiger.com/news/2613766024)

**Concentration in mega-cap tech**
- [BROKER DATA] Nasdaq: retail bought about $3.4tn and sold about $3.2tn of stock in H1 2025. NVDA, TSLA and PLTR were among the most traded names. Retail averaged about $62bn/day in 2025. Most retail tech buying was in NVDA, and over half of consumer-discretionary net buying was in TSLA. In 2024 NVDA became the retail favorite at about $4bn/day traded. — [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/nvda-tsla-pltr-retail-investors-traded-a-record-6-6-trillion-of-stocks-in-years-first-half); [Nasdaq: retail trading remains elevated, favorite stock shifts](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/retail-trading-remains-elevated-favorite-stock-shifts); [Nasdaq: retail activity remains elevated](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/retail-activity-remains-elevated)
- [VENUE DATA] MEMX (one venue, July 2025): TSLA was about **11.6%** of retail volume and NVDA about **6.8%**. — [MEMX Retail Trading Insights](https://memx.com/insights/retail-trading-insights)
- [SELL-SIDE] Vanda week example: retail net bought $3.1bn of stocks, led by TSLA +$961m, PLTR +$722m, AMZN +$456m and NVDA +$449m. The source page is undated. — [Nasdaq/Vanda via search summary](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/retail-fading-rally)
- [SELL-SIDE] Vanda (early 2026): $706m of net retail inflows into NVDA over 5 days. NVDA stayed the "retail king" despite lagging in 2026. — [The Wealth Advisor](https://www.thewealthadvisor.com/article/nvidia-stock-retains-crown-retail-king-despite-lagging-2026)
- [COMMENTARY] Reports of retail rotating out of the Mag 7 into SK Hynix and Marvell, and of record NVDA selling. — [Sherwood: retail bailing on Nvidia](https://www.sherwood.news/markets/retail-traders-are-bailing-on-nvidia-selling-the-most-in-a-decade); [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/retail-traders-rotate-from-mag-seven-into-sk-hynix-marvell)

**2026**
- [MARKET-MAKER DATA] Citadel Securities: from 2 Jan to 13 Feb 2026, average daily retail dollar demand on its platform was about **25% above the 2021 peak**. — [Citadel Securities, "retail crowd buying dips at record pace"](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/news-and-insights/in_the_news/citadel-securities-sees-retail-crowd-buying-dips-at-record-pace/)
- [MARKET-MAKER DATA] Citadel 1H 2026 Market Structure & Flows: retail bought **about 3.5x the average daily amount on SPX down days** in 1H26, "the strongest buy-the-dip behavior in our dataset". **12 June 2026** was the largest single day of retail net buying on its platform. These figures come from the search summary; the page returned 403 when fetched. — [Citadel Securities 1H 2026](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/?p=43645)
- [SELL-SIDE] In the 2026 software selloff, hedge funds shorted software at a record pace while retail bought at a record pace. — [itiger](https://www.itiger.com/news/1174403587); [oninvest](https://en.oninvest.com/article/retail-investors-bought-back-shares-of-software-developers-at-a-record-pace-on-the-downturn)
- [SELL-SIDE] JPM: on the first trading day after the March 2026 Middle East conflict began, retail put **$2.2bn** into stocks and ETFs. JPM later flagged "the first persistent signs of weakness": weekly purchases slowed by about 30% after a near-record February. — [Sherwood News](https://www.sherwood.news/markets/jpmorgan-warns-on-the-first-persistent-signs-of-weakness-from-retail-traders/)

**2020 baseline (academic)**
- [ACADEMIC] Welch (JF 2022): Robinhood investors *increased* holdings in the March 2020 crash ("absence of collective panic and margin calls") and were rewarded in the rebound. Holdings tilted toward high-volume, mostly large stocks. The aggregate consensus portfolio (mid-2018 to mid-2020) showed good timing and alpha. — [NBER w27866](https://www.nber.org/papers/w27866); [JF citation](https://ideas.repec.org:443/a/bla/jfinan/v77y2022i3p1489-1527.html)
- Note the conflict with JPM's characterization above (JPM: individuals sold on 2020 down days; Welch: Robinhood users added). The two use different populations: all retail versus Robinhood app users.

### Inferences
- "Buy the dip in mega-cap tech" became a conditioned crowd reflex, reinforced by V-shaped recoveries in 2020, 2022–23 and 2025. Because retail demand arrives *on* down days, it probably dampens selloff depth in NVDA, TSLA and the index. The flip side is that a regime where dips do not recover (2000–02 or 2008 style) would hit a crowd that is long, concentrated and partly leveraged.
- Retail flow is a liquidity and price-support factor rather than an information signal at the index level. Its main use for a systematic strategy is to watch for *changes* (for example JPM's 30% deceleration) rather than levels.

### Gaps
- No direct access to JPM, Vanda or Citadel primary reports (paywalled or 403). Numbers come via press, and dates sometimes conflict (3 vs 4 April).
- No retail-flow data for H2 2026 (Aug–Nov) was found.
- No dated, Nasdaq-sourced NVDA/TSLA share-of-retail-volume time series. Pull this from Nasdaq Data Link RTAT directly.

## Q2. Do sentiment extremes (AAII bull-bear, Fear & Greed, put-call, NAAIM) predict market or mega-cap returns out-of-sample?

### Takeaway
Survey and composite sentiment indicators have weak and unstable standalone predictive power at short horizons. The best-supported version is the contrarian signal after *extreme pessimism*, at 6–12 month horizons. Extreme bullishness predicts little: roughly a coin flip. The academic evidence that holds up out of sample is for *constructed* sentiment indices (Baker-Wurgler, aligned PLS sentiment) at monthly or longer horizons, not for AAII or Fear & Greed thresholds. No peer-reviewed out-of-sample test of CNN Fear & Greed or NAAIM was found.

### Cited Findings
- [ACADEMIC] Huang, Jiang, Tu & Zhou (RFS 2015), "Investor Sentiment Aligned": a PLS-aligned version of the Baker-Wurgler proxies (1965–2010, 546 months) predicts the market both in and out of sample. Its OOS R² is reported to be more than 10x Baker-Wurgler's (secondary claim). High sentiment predicts *low* future returns. It also predicts cross-sectional returns (size, value, momentum, industry). — [IDEAS/RFS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/rfinst/v28y2015i3p791-837..html); [WUSTL profile](https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/investor-sentiment-aligned-a-powerful-predictor-of-stock-returns/); [Alpha Architect summary](https://alphaarchitect.com/2013/11/predicting-stock-returns-with-investor-sentiment/)
- [ACADEMIC, via secondary] SF Fed work (as summarized by Option Alpha): sentiment alone predicts weakly, and sentiment combined with momentum predicts 1-month returns better. Even at extreme bullish readings, the market was up about half the time. — [Option Alpha](https://optionalpha.com/podcast/predictive-power-of-investor-sentiment)
- [PUBLISHER STUDY] AAII (Rotblut, 1987–2018): unusually *low* optimism has a stronger contrarian record than high optimism. AAII has an obvious interest in this conclusion. — [AAII Sentiment Survey](https://www.aaii.com/sentimentsurvey?a=blog20190321); [AAII Investor Update](https://www.aaii.com/files/investorupdate/20190103.html)
- [PRACTITIONER] Schaeffer's (Sept 2025, data since 1990): SPX average returns were best when the 4-week average AAII bull-bear spread was below −10%. — [Schaeffer's](https://www.schaeffersresearch.com/content/analysis/2025/09/24/aaii-bearish-tilt-could-be-a-reliable-contrarian-indicator)
- [PRACTITIONER] Skeptical: concludes AAII has limited predictive value. — [Options Stocks Machines](https://optionstocksmachines.substack.com/p/botw-4-aaiisignal-or-noise). Small-sample example: 8 instances of bears ≥ 56% (Australian index). — [greenbackd](https://greenbackd.com/tag/aaii)
- [PRACTITIONER] CNN Fear & Greed decile backtest on SPY (9,000+ trades, 1–21 day holds). The best bucket was a reading of 20–40 with a 15–21 day hold. For a reading of 20–30 with a 21-day hold, the average was +1.45% with a 70.7% win rate. The author says "buy fear, sell greed" is oversimplified. The index has 7 inputs, including the put/call ratio, junk-bond demand, VIX and safe-haven demand. — [Backtest Substack](https://backtest.substack.com/p/i-backtested-the-cnn-fear-and-greed)
- [VENDOR] SentimenTrader: extreme readings were useful historically but "less reliable recently". Forward returns are muted after extreme confidence. — [SentimenTrader](https://www.sentimentrader.com/blog/we-havent-seen-extremes-like-this-in-15-years)
- Caveat on F&G backtests: long-history TradingView strategies use a *reconstructed* index, not the official CNN series, which only began around 2011–12. — [TradingView script](https://www.tradingview.com/script/SKEvwrNo)

### Inferences
- Treat extreme fear or bearishness as a weak tilt toward 1–12 month equity exposure, and do not treat extreme greed as a sell signal. Post-2020 retail behavior (Q1) means fear readings now arrive alongside heavy retail dip-buying, which may shorten the window for contrarian edge.
- Signals with tiny independent episode counts (for example ≥ 56% AAII bears) are not robust. Overlapping daily observations inflate apparent n.
- For mega-cap tech specifically, no study links AAII or F&G to NVDA/TSLA forward returns. Any use for single names is an extrapolation.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed out-of-sample test of AAII thresholds, CNN F&G, NAAIM exposure index or equity-only put/call for 2020–2026 was found.
- NAAIM was not covered by any source retrieved.
- The predictive power of the put/call ratio cannot be separated from the F&G composite using these sources.

## Q3. Does 0DTE / dealer hedging flow create predictable intraday or next-day patterns, or is it noise?

### Takeaway
0DTE now dominates SPX options volume (about 59% in 2025, with record months above 61%), and retail is about half of it. The best academic evidence (Dim, Eraker & Vilkov) finds 0DTE gamma is *not* destabilizing. Aggregate 0DTE gamma is inversely related to intraday realized volatility (dealers are net long gamma and trade against moves), and effects do not carry overnight or into the next day. Predictable patterns exist mainly at the close, from mechanical LETF rebalancing and option delta-hedging (Barbon et al.), and they are short-lived. A next-day predictive edge from 0DTE flow is not supported.

### Cited Findings
- [EXCHANGE DATA] Cboe: 0DTE was about **59% of SPX options volume in 2025** (an annual record, about 2.3m contracts/day). It reached a **61% record share in May 2025** on a "retail resurgence". Retail is about 50–60% of 0DTE flow, dipping to 47% in April 2025. — [Cboe: 0DTE 61% share](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/spx-0-dte-options-jump-to-61-share-on-retail-resurgence); [Cboe: 0DTEs Decoded](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/0-dt-es-decoded-positioning-trends-and-market-impact); [SpotGamma](https://spotgamma.com/record-0dte-volume-reshapes-the-sp-500/); [FOW](https://www.fow.com/insights/analysis-cboe-points-to-retail-flow-as-zero-day-options-grow)
- One secondary source cites a 66% share in July 2026. Not verified against Cboe. — [Resonanz Capital](https://resonanzcapital.com/insights/every-day-the-market-hires-a-counter-trend-trader)
- [ACADEMIC] Dim, Eraker & Vilkov (2024), "0DTEs: Trading, Gamma Risk and Volatility Propagation" (SSRN 4692190). High 0DTE open-interest gamma does *not* propagate past volatility. Aggregate 0DTE gamma is *inversely* related to intraday realized volatility. Effects do not extend overnight or to lagged volatility. Volume shocks are followed by higher underlying volume. 0DTEs carry the largest volatility risk premium. — [Harbourfront Quant summary](https://harbourfrontquant.substack.com/p/the-rise-of-0dte-options-cause-for); [Harbourfront: impact of 0DTE](https://harbourfrontquant.substack.com/p/impact-of-zero-dte-options-on-the)
- [ACADEMIC, 2026 via commentary] Heavy same-day option activity is negatively related to realized volatility, consistent with dealers who are long gamma trading counter-trend. — [Resonanz Capital](https://resonanzcapital.com/insights/every-day-the-market-hires-a-counter-trend-trader)
- [ACADEMIC thesis] A Princeton thesis finds 0DTE trading is related to intraday volatility, but the evidence does not support gamma-driven dealer hedging as the mechanism. — [Princeton thesis](https://theses-dissertations.princeton.edu/entities/publication/61115cd1-cb09-4a8b-9230-ff488fc2a386)
- [ACADEMIC] Barbon et al.: LETF rebalancing and options delta-hedging cause end-of-day momentum and mean-reversion. LETF-driven price effects attract liquidity and are shorter-lived. — [SSRN 3925725](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=3925725)

### Inferences
- The popular "gamma squeeze / 0DTE crash" narrative (commentary) is not supported as a systematic effect. In long-gamma regimes, 0DTE hedging dampens intraday ranges. Any edge is intraday, near the close, capacity-limited, and competed for by HFT and market makers. For a daily or weekly mega-cap strategy, treat 0DTE flow as noise apart from a possible volatility-regime feature (dealer gamma sign).
- Dealer gamma *sign* (long versus short) is commercially estimated (SpotGamma and others) and not observed directly. Backtests built on vendor gamma estimates inherit model error.

### Gaps
- Exact coefficients and sample periods from Dim-Eraker-Vilkov were not verified against the paper (only summaries were read).
- No rigorous study of 0DTE flow in single-stock mega-caps (NVDA/TSLA weeklies and daily expiries) was found.

## Q4. Capitulation signals (volume spikes, breadth washouts, VIX > 30), meme/herding episodes and leveraged single-stock ETFs: evidence on subsequent returns and robustness

### Takeaway
VIX > 30 has historically been followed by above-average 12-month S&P returns (median roughly +18–22% vs about +10–11% unconditional), but with large dispersion and notable failures (2008). The edge is weak or absent below 6 months, and the published stats rely on overlapping daily observations from non-academic sources. Retail *herding* into attention stocks predicts *negative* short-term returns (Barber et al.). Leveraged single-stock ETFs are about 90% retail, add close-concentrated pro-cyclical flow, and carry a structural rebalancing drag.

### Cited Findings
**VIX > 30 / capitulation**
- [INDEX PROVIDER] S&P Indexology (March 2022): of 557 days with VIX > 30, **88%** were followed by positive 12-month S&P 500 returns. — [Indexology](https://www.indexologyblog.com/2022/03/15/markets-remained-volatile-but-no-need-to-panic/)
- [PRACTITIONER] Since 1990: median 12-month forward return **+22.4%** with VIX > 30 vs +11.3% with VIX < 15 (722 observations). Above 45, median +30.6% with a 96.4% hit rate. — [eco3min Contrarian's Almanac](https://eco3min.fr/en/vix-contrarian-almanac-dataset/); [eco3min Q&A](https://eco3min.fr/en/qa/vix-contrarian-buy-signal/)
- [PRACTITIONER] Using *independent crossings* rather than days: 24 crossings above 30, of which 18 (about 75%) had positive 12-month returns. 2008 was −10.8% at 12 months. The median turns positive only from the 6-month horizon onward. VIX > 30 together with backwardation is more informative than the level alone. — [eco3min thresholds](https://eco3min.fr/en/vix-contrarian-buy-signal-historical-thresholds-equity-rebound/)
- [PRACTITIONER] Elm Wealth (2026), "When Fear Spikes Should You Buy?", a more skeptical treatment from a quant-leaning manager (full results not extracted). — [Elm Wealth PDF](https://elmwealth.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Elm-When-Fear-Spikes-Should-You-Buy.pdf); [Advisor Perspectives](https://www.advisorperspectives.com/articles/2026/08/10/fear-spikes-should-buy)
- The jump in hit rate from 88% (557 days) to about 75% (24 crossings) shows how much overlapping daily samples inflate the apparent evidence.

**Herding / meme episodes**
- [ACADEMIC] Barber, Huang, Odean & Schwarz, "Attention-Induced Trading and Returns: Evidence from Robinhood Users" (JF 2022). The top stocks bought by Robinhood herds have an average 5-day abnormal return of about **−3%**, and about **−6%** for the most extreme herding. Herding is driven by app display and attention (e.g. "Top Movers"). Outages cut retail trading more in high-attention stocks. Short selling rises in herding stocks, consistent with sophisticated traders taking the other side. — [paper PDF](https://iimb.ac.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/attention-induced-trading.pdf); [UC Davis](https://gsm.ucdavis.edu/news/what-effect-brokerage-robinhood-having-stock-market); [Evidence Investor](https://www.evidenceinvestor.com/why-most-robinhood-traders-earn-lousy-returns/)
- [ACADEMIC] Boehmer, Jones, Zhang & Zhang (JF 2021): the sub-penny price-improvement method identifies marketable retail trades in TAQ. Retail order imbalance carries firm-level information that predicts returns (described by the authors as "suggestive"). This is challenged by Barber, Huang, Jorion, Odean & Schwarz (JF 2024), who place 85k test trades and find the method captures less than a third of retail trades and misclassifies some, and by Ardia et al. (2024), whose recent-period replication reaches "different conclusions". — [RePEc JF 2021](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:jfinan:v:76:y:2021:i:5:p:2249-2305); [TCU / identifying market maker trades](https://www.neeley.tcu.edu/centers/lkcm-center-for-financial-studies/finance-conference/identifying-market-maker-trades); [Alpha Architect on TAQ tracking](https://medium.com/@alphaarchitect/how-to-track-retail-investor-activity-in-taq-6eeba4d614bd)
- Reconciling the papers: aggregate or slow-moving retail portfolios can do fine (Welch, BJZZ), while attention-driven *herding spikes* reverse (Barber et al.). The two do not contradict each other.

**Leveraged single-stock ETFs (TSLL, NVDL, etc.)**
- [SELL-SIDE] Direxion / Vanda / The Compound (2026, via Reuters): individuals are about **90%** of leveraged-ETF trading while being about 5.5% of overall US equity trading. Total leveraged-product assets are about $160.5bn. Single-stock products are about half of leveraged equity fund volume. The largest are tied to TSLA, MU, META, PLTR and NVDA. — [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/etfs/leveraged-etfs-ride-retail-trading-boom-as-single-stock-bets-redefine-market-stress-playbook/265389); [Ultumus](https://insights.ultumus.com/feed/leveraged-etfs-have-stopped-being-a-niche-trade.-the-charts-prove-it); [Finviz/Direxion](https://finviz.com/news/328688/exclusive-liquidity-begets-liquidity-direxion-on-the-retail-surge-in-leveraged-single-stock-etfs)
- [ACADEMIC] Bessembinder (Aug 2025), "Leveraged Single-Stock ETFs": frictionless returns underperform the simple leveraged benchmark by 0.28% on average for 3x and 0.49% for −2x, due to daily rebalancing. — [Alpha Architect](https://alphaarchitect.com/leveraged-single-stock-etfs/)
- [ACADEMIC/CENTRAL BANK] LETF rebalancing is always in the direction of the day's move, concentrated near the close, and can amplify moves on the most vulnerable days. Single-stock versions concentrate this into one name. Evidence on market-wide impact is mixed, and contrarian LETF investors partially offset it. — [Bank Underground](https://bankunderground.co.uk/2023/08/31/leveraged-and-inverse-etfs-the-exotic-side-of-exchange-traded-funds/amp/); [AMRO](https://amro-asia.org/do-leveraged-etfs-make-markets-more-volatile)
- [COMMENTARY] Leveraged single-stock ETFs topped 2026 performance charts, with warnings about volatility decay in choppy markets. — [TheStreet Pro](https://pro.thestreet.com/posts/the-hottest-etfs-of-2026-come-with-a-dangerous-warning-label)

### Inferences
- A defensible rule: VIX > 30 (ideally with term-structure backwardation) is a *modest* positive tilt for 6–12 month equity exposure. Expect about 1 in 4 crossings to fail at 12 months, and do not size it as a high-conviction signal. Out-of-sample n is about 2 dozen independent episodes since 1990.
- In NVDA and TSLA, LETF rebalancing plus retail dip-buying creates two-sided close-of-day flow. Late-day momentum on big-move days is the plausible microstructure effect. It is short-lived and arbitraged.
- Spikes in retail attention or herding in a single name (top-mover lists, social volume) are a short-horizon *negative* signal. Strong aggregate retail dip-buying in mega-caps has not been shown to be a negative signal.

### Gaps
- No academic study of breadth-washout or volume-spike capitulation signals (e.g. 90% down-volume days) for 2020–2026 with out-of-sample tests was retrieved.
- No study measures TSLL/NVDL rebalancing impact on TSLA/NVDA closing prints.
- No rigorous post-mortem of the 2021 GME/AMC meme episode's return predictability was retrieved in this pass. The Barber et al. herding results cover the mechanism generically.
- Full Elm Wealth results were not extracted.
