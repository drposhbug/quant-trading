# SpaceX and Musk-Ecosystem Tradability (status as of 2026-11-18)

Research date: 2026-11-18. Most sources are secondary (news and aggregators). I did not open the S-1 or 424(b)(4) on EDGAR directly; figures marked "verify" should be checked there before modeling.

## 1. Has SpaceX IPO'd? Ticker, exchange, date, float, lockups, options

### Takeaway
Yes. SpaceX (Space Exploration Technologies Corp, which absorbed xAI in Feb 2026) has been public on Nasdaq as **SPCX** since **2026-06-12**. The IPO priced at $135 with a float of about 5%. Listed options have traded since 2026-06-16. The stock joined the Nasdaq-100 on 2026-07-07. The main 180-day lockup expires around **2026-12-08**. Retail can now buy the common stock and options directly, so proxies are mostly obsolete for price exposure.

### Cited Findings
**Filing and listing**
- 2026-04: confidential IPO filing — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/spacex-stock-price-ipo-spcx-initial-public-offering/)
- 2026-05-15: Nasdaq chosen as the venue. 2026-05-20: public S-1 filed. Roadshow and subscription ran 2026-06-03 to 06-11 — [Wikipedia: IPO of SpaceX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_public_offering_of_SpaceX)
- Some reports date the S-1 to May 21 rather than May 20 — [investinglive](https://investinglive.com/stocks/spacex-files-for-nasdaq-ipo-with-musk-retaining-851-voting-control-20260520/); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/spacex-s1-prospectus-filed-nasdaq-spcx-record-ipo)
- Class A shares listed on Nasdaq (and Nasdaq Texas). Dual class: Class B carries 10 votes per share. SpaceX is a "controlled company" under Nasdaq rules — [investinglive, 2026-05-20](https://investinglive.com/stocks/spacex-files-for-nasdaq-ipo-with-musk-retaining-851-voting-control-20260520/)

**Pricing and first day**
- Priced 2026-06-11 at $135. First trade 2026-06-12: opened at $150, intraday high $176.52, closed at $160.95 (+19%). About $75B raised, the largest IPO ever — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/spacex-stock-price-ipo-spcx-initial-public-offering/); [NPR, 2026-06-12](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/12/nx-s1-5855004/stock-ai-spacex-ipo-elon-musk)

- Offering terms: 555,555,555 Class A shares plus an 83,333,333-share greenshoe (15%). Valuation was $1.77T at the offer price. Float was about 5% (the usual 10% minimum was waived). Allocation was 70:30 institutional to retail. Leads were Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, in a 23-bank syndicate. Fees were under 0.75% — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_public_offering_of_SpaceX)
- Gross proceeds conflict: Wikipedia's intro says $86B (citing BBC), while most outlets say $75B. 555.6M × $135 = $75.0B before the greenshoe, which suggests the $86B figure includes the greenshoe. That is my inference; verify it in the 424(b)(4).

### Inferences
- The tiny float (~5%) plus staggered unlocks make supply shocks the dominant event risk. The 2026-12-08 expiry is the next big one, about 0.8B shares. That is larger than the entire IPO share count (555.6M + greenshoe 83.3M = ~639M).
- An IV of about 55–70% means SPCX options price roughly 2× TSLA-like vol regimes. Vol-selling strategies need to account for event clustering: earnings, lockups and merger news.

### Inferences
- Rough stake value at 2026-11-17: 551.2M × $141.70 ≈ $78B, against a GOOGL market cap in the multi-trillions, so a low-single-digit-% sensitivity. That makes it useless as a SpaceX tracker. It does matter for modeling GOOGL earnings noise: unrealized gains and losses flow through "other income" every quarter.
- DXYZ: model it as SPCX × ~0.1 plus Anthropic, plus other privates, plus a premium/discount process. Tracking error is dominated by the premium/discount.

### Gaps

## 3. TSLA–SPCX co-movement and Musk-ecosystem correlations

### Takeaway
The correlation is regime-dependent and has been driven by merger news. 20-day daily-return correlation was about 0.10–0.15 in Jun–Jul 2026 and about 0.64–0.81 in Oct 2026. Only about 0.32 of it remains after removing Nasdaq-100 moves. A Tesla–SpaceX merger is under active exploration, with Bloomberg reporting a possible announcement as soon as December 2026. That would turn the pair into a merger-arb spread.

## 4. New-IPO quant considerations (short history, lockups, index inclusion)

### Takeaway
SPCX has only about 5 months (about 110 sessions) of history. Its supply is set by lockup tranches and index rule changes rather than organic flow. Excluded from the S&P 500 until it has 12 months of trading and four profitable quarters, it could become eligible around mid/late 2027 at the earliest. A merger with TSLA would short-circuit that.

### Cited Findings
**Index inclusion**
- Nasdaq-100 fast entry came from a 2026-03-30 rule change allowing inclusion after 15 trading days. Eligibility was confirmed 2026-06-26, and SPCX joined before the open on 2026-07-07 — [Yahoo/Quartz](https://qz.com/spacex-nasdaq-100-index-inclusion-ipo-062926); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91566621/spacex-stock-nasdaq-100-timeline-date-impact-qqq-401k)
- Its float-adjusted Nasdaq-100 weight is about 1%. Some sources mention a 3× multiplier — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_public_offering_of_SpaceX)
- CRSP US Total Market added it after 5 trading days. Russell and FTSE applied early-inclusion rule changes, with Russell 1000 inclusion within about a week. S&P kept its rules (12 months trading plus profitability), reaffirmed 2026-06-04 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_public_offering_of_SpaceX); [SpotGamma](https://spotgamma.com/spacex-ipo-index-changes-spotgamma/)
- Stock "falling anyway" after joining the Nasdaq-100 — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/spacex-officially-joined-nasdaq-100-112000427.html)

**Event clustering**
- The October lockup release two days after earnings coincided with a reported -9.1% day — [StockAlarm](https://pro.stockalarm.io/blog/spacex-ipo-lockup-financials)
- The bond deal after the IPO hit the stock — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_public_offering_of_SpaceX)
- Options IV fell from about 169% at launch to about 50–70% — [Saxo](https://www.home.saxo/content/articles/options/when-the-chain-opens-trading-options-on-the-fresh-spacex-spcx-listing-17062026); [OptionCharts](https://optioncharts.io/options/SPCX)

### Inferences
- Event calendar to encode:
  - 2026-12-08: main lockup expiry
  - Possible merger announcement in Dec 2026 or early 2027
  - Q4 earnings, around Jan–Feb 2027
  - About 2027-06-12: Musk and insider lockup ends
  - The extended lockup after Q2-2027 results
  - S&P 500 eligibility no earlier than about 2027-06 plus profitability
- With about 110 daily bars, vol and beta estimates have wide confidence intervals. Shrink toward a prior, such as a peer basket of TSLA, QQQ and space names, or use intraday data to raise the sample size.
- Pre-IPO "history" only exists as tender and secondary marks (DXYZ NAV, Forge/EquityZen, GOOGL's 10-Q marks). These are not tradable daily series and should not be spliced into the return series.
- Survivorship: academic IPO-lockup studies show negative abnormal returns around expiry. SPCX's float is far smaller than typical, though, so the effect size may differ; treat it as a hypothesis to test.
