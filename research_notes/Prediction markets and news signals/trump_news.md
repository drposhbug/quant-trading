# Trump posts, policy news and US equities: market impact and how to follow it in near-real-time (as of 2026-11-18)

Scope: how much and how fast Trump posts (Truth Social and X) and White House policy news move TSLA, SPCX and the mega-cap tech names; whether the effect is fading; and which free or cheap data sources a retail quant can use. Research ran 2026-11-18. Several key pages (Axios, QUT/The Conversation, SAN) returned 403 errors or redirects, so some figures come from search-engine summaries or secondary sources. These are flagged where they appear.

## Q1. Event-study evidence: how big is the reaction, how fast, and does it reverse?

### Takeaway
Academic studies of the 2016-2020 tweets agree on the main points. Company-specific and economy-related tweets moved prices, volume and volatility within minutes to the same trading day, but the price effect mostly reversed within a few trading days. Some studies also find the market had already moved *before* the tweet. The 2025-2026 Truth Social episodes were much larger, but they were policy *actions* (tariff pause, export licenses) or a political feud, not just words. Single-name moves in those episodes ran from about 3% to 19% in a day.

### Cited Findings
**Academic, Trump-era tweets (2016-2020)**
- Ge, Kurov & Wolfe (J. Financial Research, 2019) studied Trump tweets from 2016-11-09 to 2017-07-31 that named a listed company. The tweets moved the stock and raised volume, volatility and institutional attention, but "the initial price impact is reversed in the next few trading days". Effects were stronger before the inauguration. — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfnres/v42y2019i2p213-242.html); [CORE pdf](https://core.ac.uk/download/235415754.pdf)
- Machus, Mestel & Theissen (J. Behavioral & Experimental Finance, 2022) used minute-by-minute data. The tweets raised trading activity but had no lasting price effect. They also found abnormal returns, volume and attention *before* the tweets. — [IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/beexfi/v33y2022ics2214635021001386.html); [Mannheim pdf](https://madoc.bib.uni-mannheim.de/62106/1/1-s2.0-S2214635021001386-main.pdf)
- A high-frequency study of economy-related tweets (SSRN 3840203) found Trump's tweets were "most often a reaction to pre-existing market movements". It used non-tweet windows matched for intraday seasonality. (Abstract-level reading only.) — [Frankfurt repository pdf](https://publikationen.ub.uni-frankfurt.de/files/59156/SSRN-id3840203.pdf)
- An earlier company-tweet event study in *Algorithmic Finance* (2017) found abnormal returns on the event day, almost entirely between that day's open and close. — [Sage / Algorithmic Finance](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3233/AF-170211)
- Ajjoub, Walker & Zhao (2020): the effect depends on the type of firm. For media firms, positive tweets moved prices up. For non-media firms, negative tweets went with significant declines. (Summary from search results.) — [Emerald IJMF](https://emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/IJMF-02-2020-0068/full/html)
- A Princeton undergraduate thesis (2026, not peer-reviewed) used intraday SPY/QQQ data. Higher volatility before an event went with more negative returns afterwards, which suggests reversal dynamics. — [Princeton theses](https://theses-dissertations.princeton.edu/entities/publication/b3506b16-95f9-437c-82f0-3480f60066af)
- Scharnowski (J. Financial Research, 2026) studies social media, HFT and after-hours market making around Trump tweets. Found in search; I did not extract its findings. — [Mannheim pdf](https://madoc.bib.uni-mannheim.de/71952/1/J%20of%20Financial%20Research%20-%202026%20-%20Scharnowski%20-%20Social%20media%20high%E2%80%90frequency%20trading%20and%20market%20making%20after%E2%80%90hours%20.pdf)

**2025-2026 episodes (dated, mostly single-event and anecdotal)**

Hours later he announced a 90-day pause with a 10% baseline tariff (China excluded). At the close the S&P 500 was up about 9.5%, its best day since 2008, and the Nasdaq Composite was up about 12.2%, its best since 2001. In the four prior sessions the S&P had fallen about 12%. — [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/equities/5-year-record-surge-for-us-stocks-as-trump-presses-pause-on-some-tariffs/260062); [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/31808646/stocks-spike-as-trump-announces-90-day-tariff-pause); [ThinkAdvisor](https://www.thinkadvisor.com/amp/2025/04/09/stocks-surge-most-in-5-years/)
- **2025-04-15/16, NVDA H20 export license requirement.** NVDA fell as much as about 10% to near $100. Nvidia flagged a roughly $5.5B charge. — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/asia/2025/04/16/nvidia-shares-trump-chip-ban-h20-china/); [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/nvidia-plunges-10-as-tuesday-selling-picks-up-steam-on-china-chip-curbs-202504161950)

NVDA rose about 5%. — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2025/07/16/nvidia-stock-investors-got-great-news-trump-admin); [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/articles/nvidias-ceo-says-it-has-approval-to-sell-its-h20-ai-computer-chips-in-china-2025-07-14). A Gulf News headline claims NVDA rose 18.72% on this; that is probably a multi-week figure, so treat it as unreliable as a one-day move. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/nvidia-stocks-up-1872-as-trump-retreats-from-h20-chip-ban-after-dinner-with-jensen-huang-1.500089151)
- **2025-05-23, Apple and India.** Trump's Truth Social post threatened a 25% tariff on iPhones not made in the US ("not India, or anyplace else"). AAPL fell up to about 3.9% after the open and was about 2.8% lower at midday ($195.76). The same day's 50% EU tariff threat moved the whole market, which confounds the single-name effect. — [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/equities/trump-threatens-25-tariffs-against-apple-unless-iphones-made-in-us/260639); [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/apple-stock-sinks-below-200-after-trump-threatens-more-tariffs-202505231255); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/05/23/us-made-iphone-trump-tariffs-apple-india-china-production-tim-cook/)
- **2025-06-05, Trump-Musk feud.** TSLA closed down about 14% (14.3% per TipRanks), losing about $150-153B of market value on a day with no company news. Trump suggested ending federal contracts with Musk's companies, and Musk threatened to decommission Dragon. One blog's "$380B" figure is an outlier and not credible. — [RNZ/Reuters](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/top/563313/trump-musk-row-slams-tesla-shares-us-150-billion-in-market-value-wiped-out); [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/tesla-stock-loses-153b-amid-musk-trump-feud-musk-says-will-decommission-dragon-spacecraft); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/musk-trump-feud-tesla-market-cap-loss-ev-credits-spacex-contracts-125060600160_1.html)
- **SPCX (SpaceX).** The IPO priced at $135 and listed on Nasdaq on 2026-06-12. It opened at $150, closed at $160.95 (+19.2%) and hit an intraday high of $176.52. Options listed shortly after. A late-August 2026 disclosure that Trump bought SPCX shares in June coincided with a close of $135 (-1.44%). Commentary says federal launch approvals and contracts are the real policy channel. Sources disagree on the IPO size ($75B vs about $85.7B). — [capital.com](https://capital.com/en-int/market-updates/spacex-ipo-news-15-06-2026); [ts2.tech](https://ts2.tech/en/spacex-stock-rises-as-trump-disclosure-sharpens-government-ties-risk/); [ROIC](https://www.roic.ai/news/spacex-options-to-begin-trading-on-tuesday-after-ipo-06-12-2026)
- **Iran-war posts (2026).** An Axios review of 269 Iran-related posts reportedly found average 5-minute futures moves of 0.73% after posts, against 0.2% for a typical 5-minute window, and the effect diminished over time. I could not read the Axios original (403); these figures come from a secondary crypto-news summary. — [CoinEdition summary](https://coinedition.com/how-trumps-truth-social-posts-moved-bitcoin-stocks-and-oil-in-2025-and-2026/); original [Axios 2026-04-22](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/22/trump-stocks-posts-iran)
- **Pre-positioning before posts.** QUT researchers (Graham, Choarzy, Harrington; published via The Conversation, around March 2026) found 15 events in a 73-day window with unusual oil-futures trading in the minutes before Trump posts, including one move about 20 minutes before a post. They put the chance of patterns this extreme at "one in a billion" and estimate tens to hundreds of millions of dollars in profits. This was oil futures, not equities, and is not peer-reviewed. — [QUT](https://www.qut.edu.au/news/realfocus/the-market-moves-before-trump-posts); [The Conversation](https://stories.theconversation.com/the-market-moves-before-trump-posts/); [event chart](https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/6EmzW/4/). A WSJ piece also covers well-timed trades just before policy surprises. — [WSJ via Tiger](https://www.itiger.com/news/2622858358)

### Inferences
- Speed: futures and liquid ETFs reprice within seconds to a few minutes (the 5-minute Axios windows, minute-level academic data). A retail trader acting on a post after reading it is almost certainly after the first move.
- Reversal: the academic consensus is that pure "talk" tweets partly or fully reverse within days. Posts that come with an actual policy action (the 2025-04-09 pause, H20 licensing) produced lasting repricing. A useful split is "words" vs "signed action".
- Both the old and new evidence show pre-tweet abnormal activity. A strategy keyed off the public post timestamp will often trade against people who were already positioned.

### Gaps
- I found no peer-reviewed event study specific to Truth Social and single equities for 2025-2026. Most of the 2025-26 evidence is single-event news coverage.
- I could not verify the posting minute of the 2025-04-09 "great time to buy" post (reported as 9:37 ET in other coverage I could not open) or the 1:18 pm ET pause announcement. Check against the trumpstruth.org timestamps.
- I did not extract numbers from Scharnowski (2026) or SSRN 3840203 beyond the abstracts.

## Q2. Has the market impact faded or grown? The "TACO" trade

### Takeaway
The evidence points to fading sensitivity to routine posts. Markets increasingly separate posts from signed orders, and the "TACO" (Trump Always Chickens Out) expectation dampens the selloffs that follow tariff threats. Rare high-stakes posts still move markets hard.

### Cited Findings
- "TACO" describes buying the dip after tariff threats and selling after the pullback or delay. April 2025 is the canonical case: the S&P fell more than 10% in three days after Liberation Day, then a 90-day pause came about a week later. — [Wikipedia: Trump Always Chickens Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_Always_Chickens_Out); [Babypips explainer, 2025-10-16](https://www.babypips.com/news/headlines-2025-10-16-trump-taco-trade-explainer)
- CNN (2025-07-09): if investors broadly bet that Trump will blink, there is no market panic to force a reversal. Raymond James' Ed Mills called this "a dangerous game when you need a market reaction to get a policy change". — [CNN](https://us.cnn.com/2025/07/09/business/trump-tariffs-markets-taco)
- Fortune (2025-07-12): the TACO trade may be "backfiring", with stocks at record highs while the trade war continued. — [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2025/07/12/trump-tariffs-taco-trade-backfiring-stock-market-record-highs-trade-war)
- Axios (2026-07-21), "Bond traders are tuning out Trump posts": most Middle East posts did not move 30-minute rates volatility much, but a handful went well beyond typical levels. (Snippet only, original 403.) — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/21/trump-bonds-social-media-traders)
- An Axios review of the Iran posts found the effect diminishing over time, and traders now separate online announcements from signed orders and actual implementation. (Secondary summary.) — [CoinEdition](https://coinedition.com/how-trumps-truth-social-posts-moved-bitcoin-stocks-and-oil-in-2025-and-2026/)
- An IG piece (2026-03-24), "Trump TACOs once more", describes a stock rally and a fast oil drop on a Truth Social post during the Iran tensions. — [IG](https://www.ig.com/en/news-and-trade-ideas/trump-tacos-once-more---will-it-work-again--260324)
- JPMorgan work reported by Fortune (2025-05-02) found trading on Trump's posts has not been very profitable. A Feb 2025 study said the posts move markets "not a lot". — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/05/02/markets-swings-truth-social-posts-jpmorgan-potfolio-manager/); [Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2025/02/17/are-president-trumps-posts-moving-markets-study-shows-not-a-lot/)
- A SAN headline (2026) reads "The market largely ignores Trump's posts. These are the exceptions". I could not read the body (redirect). — [SAN](https://san.com/cc/the-market-largely-ignores-trumps-posts-these-are-the-exceptions/)

### Inferences
- Routine posts now behave like noise. Market-relevant posts are rare, and the market's reaction depends on how credible it judges the follow-through to be. Posting volume (dozens a day in reported sprees) dilutes the signal further.
- The TACO pattern means a mean-reversion bet after a tariff-threat drawdown has worked historically (April 2025), but it is now crowded and priced in. This is anecdotal: there is no systematic backtest in the sources I found.

### Gaps
- I found no systematic, dated statistic on how the average reaction per post changed from 2017 to 2026. The "fading" claim rests on Axios and secondary summaries.

## Q3. Programmatic data sources and their latency

### Takeaway
There is no free, low-latency official source for Truth Social. The official Truth API (live since 2026-08-01) is institutional and reportedly about $100k/month. The practical retail options are polling the trumpstruth.org RSS feed (delay of a few minutes), unofficial scraper APIs (seconds to minutes), Alpaca's free Benzinga news WebSocket, GDELT (15-minute batches) and RSS feeds. All of these are minutes behind HFT and institutional feeds.

### Cited Findings
**Truth Social**
- Truth Social has no official public developer API. Third parties (Scrape Creators, 1322.io) sell unofficial JSON access. These are poll-based, not push. — [Scrape Creators](https://scrapecreators.com/blog/the-best-truth-social-api-for-tracking-trump-s-posts); [1322.io guide](https://1322.io/blog/truth-social-api-guide)
- **Truth API (official, TMTG):** announced 2026-07-16 and live 2026-08-01. It is a licensed feed of the 10 most influential accounts (including Trump's, with about 12.9M followers), with an archive back to 2022. It is sold to trading firms and financial news organizations. TMTG claims delivery "within milliseconds" (not independently verified). Pricing is not public; the FT reported discussions of up to $100k/month. — [Neudata](https://www.neudata.co/blog/truth-api-truth-social-data-feed); [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2217943/there-s-now-an-api-for-mainlining-trump-s-truth-social-posts); [Arab News/Reuters](https://www.arabnews.pk/media/truth-social-to-sell-trading-firms-fastest-access-to-trumps-posts-2651269)
- Semafor (2026-08-04) describes firms paying about $100k/month for access. Spectator Australia (2026-08-04), "Trump's posts aren't alpha", argues that he posts so often that most posts get no reaction, that reactions sometimes run the opposite way (especially on repeated messages), and that alpha from the few big posts is competed away. The author concedes oil and 2026 events were not tested. — [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/08/04/2026/the-trade-in-trumps-social-media-previews); [Spectator AU](https://www.spectator.com.au/2026/08/trumps-posts-arent-alpha/)
- **trumpstruth.org** is an independent archive with a free RSS feed at `https://www.trumpstruth.org/feed`. It supports `?start_date=YYYY-MM-DD&end_date=YYYY-MM-DD` for historical exports. It checks for new posts "about every few minutes", caching can add delay, and deleted posts may be missing. — [trumpstruth.org/about](https://trumpstruth.org/about)
- The UCSB American Presidency Project offers an RSS feed for a presidential documents taxonomy (official statements; slow, not real-time). — [UCSB feed](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/taxonomy/term/199/all/feed)

**X (Twitter) API, 2026**
- Pay-per-use became the default on 2026-02-06, with no free tier for new developers. The $200/month Basic tier closed and was migrated from 2026-06-01; Pro moved after 2026-09-01. Reported price is about $0.005 per post read, with a cap of 3M reads per month (one source says 2M). These figures come from third-party summaries, not the official docs. — [Open Terms Archive](https://opentermsarchive.org/en/memos/x-replaces-its-dollar200-entry-level-api-plan-with-a-pay-per-use-model/); [GIGAZINE 2026-02-09](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260209-x-api-pay-per-use); [data365](https://data365.co/guides/twitter-api-limitations-and-pricing)

**News APIs**
- **Alpaca News API (Benzinga content):** REST history plus a real-time WebSocket, carrying about 600-900 headlines a day. The 2022 launch said it was free, with rate limits tied to the market-data plan (200 calls/min on Free). No latency figure is published. — [Alpaca docs](https://docs.alpaca.markets/docs/streaming-real-time-news); [Alpaca blog](https://alpaca.markets/blog/introducing-news-api-for-real-time-fiancial-news)
- **GDELT DOC 2.0:** free and needs no key. It updates every 15 minutes, enforces about 1 request per 5 seconds, and covers roughly the last few months. (Third-party descriptions.) — [Apify GDELT actor](https://apify.com/glitchbound/gdelt-scraper); [cloro.dev](https://cloro.dev/blog/news-api-free/)
- **Finnhub:** the free tier offers about 60 calls/min and company news with ticker tags. Real-time news over WebSocket and sentiment are premium. The license is for personal use. — [qveris.ai guide](https://qveris.ai/guides/best-free-financial-news-api)
- **NewsAPI.org:** the free Developer plan has a 24-hour delay, 100 requests/day and no production use. One 2026 source instead says a 12-hour delay and 200 credits/day, which conflicts. — [dev.to](https://dev.to/hassann/top-10-free-news-apis-for-developers-5fg8); [cloro.dev](https://cloro.dev/blog/news-api-free/)

### Inferences
- A sensible retail stack: poll the trumpstruth.org RSS every 30-60 s (politely) as the Trump-post trigger, use the Alpaca/Benzinga WebSocket for headlines naming tickers, use GDELT for broad policy themes (15-minute granularity, so only useful for slower signals), and add the whitehouse.gov and agency RSS feeds for signed orders. Expect a total delay of roughly 1-5 minutes against firms on Truth API, and the move may be done by then.
- NewsAPI's free tier is useless for trading because of the 24-hour delay.
- The X API matters less now: Trump posts first on Truth Social, and reading X is no longer free.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whitehouse.gov RSS endpoints, Reuters/AP RSS availability in 2026 (Reuters retired its public RSS years ago, unverified here), or the CNN Truth Social archive's update frequency.
- There are no independent latency measurements for trumpstruth.org, Alpaca/Benzinga or scraper APIs against the Truth Social post timestamp.
- Alpaca's free-tier news limits come from 2022 and may have changed.

## Q4. Classification approaches and tradability; historical datasets

### Takeaway
I found no rigorous public evidence that keyword or LLM classification of Trump posts produces tradable retail alpha net of latency. The sources that address it (JPMorgan, Spectator/industry commentary, the academic reversal results) suggest that any edge is either taken within seconds by faster players or is a slower drift and reversal effect. The realistic retail angle is classifying posts by *market relevance and policy credibility* (threat vs signed action) and trading the multi-day reversal, not the first minutes.

### Cited Findings
- The academic results above show the price effect of word-only tweets reverses within days (Ge et al. 2019; Machus et al. 2022), which leaves a possible fade-the-overreaction setup at retail speed. — [IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfnres/v42y2019i2p213-242.html); [IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/beexfi/v33y2022ics2214635021001386.html)
- JPMorgan: trading on Trump's posts has not been highly profitable (2025). — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/05/02/markets-swings-truth-social-posts-jpmorgan-potfolio-manager/)
- Even millisecond access may not be alpha: there are many no-reaction posts, sometimes inverse reactions, and edge is competed away (2026-08). — [Spectator AU](https://www.spectator.com.au/2026/08/trumps-posts-arent-alpha/); [AlphaMaven](https://alpha-maven.com/news/hedge-fund/trumps-posts-arent-alpha)
- Social-listening firm Infegy classified 2025 tariff discourse. For example, 98% of Truth Social posts framed higher costs as a patriotic trade-off, against 10% positive in general posts. This is descriptive, not a trading test. — [Infegy](https://www.infegy.com/insight-brief/tracking-the-2025-tariff-crisis-through-truth-social-and-social-data)
- Historical timestamped post data: trumpstruth.org RSS with date ranges covers Truth Social, and Truth API has an archive back to 2022 (institutional). The academic studies used Trump Twitter archives for 2016-2020; a UNC study used 2,148 tweets. — [trumpstruth.org](https://trumpstruth.org/about); [Neudata](https://www.neudata.co/blog/truth-api-truth-social-data-feed); [UNC Hodges](https://hodgesscholars.unc.edu/?p=11896)

### Inferences
- An LLM classifier (relevance, tickers, direction, "threat vs action") is cheap and probably more accurate than keyword rules on Trump's idiosyncratic text. But accuracy is not the bottleneck; latency and the base rate of irrelevant posts are. Use classification to filter and to label a backtest, then test returns over 1-day to 5-day horizons instead of seconds.
- For backtesting, join trumpstruth.org timestamps to minute bars, for example Alpaca's free IEX feed (which is IEX-only, so thin for minute-level events). Control for pre-post drift, which the literature finds repeatedly.

### Gaps
- I did not verify the current state of the Trump Twitter Archive (thetrumparchive.com) or the Kaggle datasets for 2016-2021 tweets. Both are commonly used, but I did not check them here.
- I found no published LLM-vs-keyword comparison on Trump posts with return outcomes.
