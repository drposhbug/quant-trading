**0 of 12 pass.**

Caveats that favour the model, so a positive ROI here is an upper bound: (1) Polymarket history is an hourly price, not an executable ask; price + 1c understates the real ask on thin brackets (the smaller accounts' brackets trade a few $k in their lifetime and live books hold tens of $ near the ask). (2) The post export is read today; in real time the tracker can lag or later backfill/delete posts, so the observed-so-far count is cleaner than what a live trader saw. (3) The rate model loses to the market on Brier and log-loss in every group, so any betting profit comes from a few cheap tail brackets, not better forecasts.

## 6. US access

The Polymarket count events are on the international Polymarket exchange (gamma/CLOB), which is close-only for US persons. Kalshi (CFTC-regulated, US-accessible) lists **KXTRUTHSOCIAL** — weekly Trump Truth Social post-count brackets since Feb 2026 (tested above as "Trump (Kalshi)"), resolved on Roll Call Factbase on a Sun–Sat ET week — plus KXTRUMPTIME ("Will Trump post this hour?") and many *MENTION speech markets; its KXELONTWEETS series ran only Mar–Apr 2025. No Musk count market currently trades on a US venue.
