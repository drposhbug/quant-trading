## 2. Method

- Odds at close t = last hourly print at or before 16:00 America/New_York (13:00 on Jul 3, Black Friday, Dec 24). Polymarket points are stamped at HH:00:02, so the read is the 15:00 ET point: up to 1 hour stale, never later than the close. Staleness can only weaken a lead, not create one.

- Predictive: r(close t → close t+1) on Δ1_t; r(close t → close t+5) on Δ5_t, sampled every 5th trading day so windows do not overlap. Same-day: r(close t−1 → close t) on Δ1_t (co-movement, not tradable).

- OLS with Newey-West t-stats (5 lags daily, 2 lags for 5-day blocks). Effect = return in bp per +10pp move in odds. Tests with < 30 usable observations are not run (listed in section 5). For very sparse series, if fewer than 30 days have Δp ≠ 0, only those days are used.

- SPCX trades only since 2026-06, so its tests are short. ANT has no prices.

- `t_ex_top5` = the same regression after dropping the 5 days with the largest |Δp| (robustness only, not part of the pass rule). `Robust` = a pass whose trimmed t is still significant at 0.05 / (number of tests).

### Chaining / rollover rules

- **FED_CUT** (Kalshi `FEDDECISION-*` / `KXFEDDECISION-*`, one event per FOMC meeting). Contract value = sum of the "Cut 25bps"
  and "Cut >25bps" legs (hourly mid of yes bid/ask; last trade when the book is one-sided; thin tail legs count as 0 until
  their first print; the series starts at the first print of the most-traded cut leg). On day t the chosen contract is the
  meeting with the earliest close time after close t, so the series always means "cut at the NEXT meeting". Kalshi closes
  each meeting's markets at ~19:00 UTC on decision day, before the 16:00 ET read, so the decision day already reads the
  following meeting.
- **RECESSION** (Kalshi `RECSSNBER-yy`, "recession in 20yy?"). Day t uses the contract for t's calendar year only
  (Jan 1 roll), even though each contract trades on until NBER/GDP data resolves it.
- **SHUTDOWN** (Kalshi `GOVSHUT-*` / `KXGOVSHUT-*`, "government shut down on <deadline>?"). Nearest deadline after close t.
  Undefined between funding fights.
- **CHINA_DEAL** (Polymarket: "Will Trump lower tariffs on China in April / by <date>", "US-China tariff agreement before
  90-day deadline / by <date>"). Nearest deadline after close t. Treated as one de-escalation series.
- **AI_GOOGLE_BEST** (Polymarket "Which company has (the) best AI model end of <month/year>", Google leg). Nearest month end.
- **NVDA_LARGEST** (Polymarket "Largest company end of <month>", NVIDIA leg). Nearest month end. Resolves on market cap,
  so it should follow NVDA, not lead it (a built-in placebo).
- **TSLA_SPACEX_MERGER**, **TSLA_ROBOTAXI** (Polymarket deadline ladders). Nearest deadline after close t.
- **Universal rule (no splicing):** Δp is always taken on ONE contract: Δ1 = p_k(close t) − p_k(close t−1) and
  Δ5 = p_k(close t) − p_k(close t−5) where k is the contract chosen on day t (Δ5 is missing if k did not trade at t−5).
  A roll never creates a jump. The contract must have a print at or before both closes and must not have closed/resolved
  (deadline = min(endDate, closedTime) on Polymarket, close_time on Kalshi). Weekend/holiday odds moves fall into the next
  trading day's Δp, which is correct: they happen before that day's close.

## 6. Caveats

- **Power is the binding constraint.** The longest series (Fed, recession) give ~800-1,100 daily observations; Polymarket thematic series give 60-470. At the Holm level the smallest detectable |corr| is ~0.15 at n = 900 and ~0.36 at n = 150 (see the MDE column), far above the ~0.02-0.05 that real daily predictors usually have. A null here rules out only LARGE leads, not small ones.

- Most daily Δp are tiny or zero outside news days (see 'Days Δp≠0'); the information is concentrated in a few days per contract, so effective sample sizes are smaller than n.

- Deadline ladders (China deal, robotaxi, Tesla-SpaceX, shutdown) decay toward 0 as a deadline nears without the event; part of Δp is that mechanical drift, not news.

- Tests are not independent: each series is tested against 13 highly correlated stocks, so one lucky stretch lights up many rows at once. The raw count of p < 0.05 hits overstates the evidence; Holm handles this conservatively.

- SHUTDOWN ends 2026-01-30: later funding fights are not listed under Kalshi KXGOVSHUT. Each funding fight gives only a few informative weeks, so its 88 five-day blocks hold only a handful of independent episodes.

- Kalshi hourly candles exist only for hours with activity; forward-filled mids can be stale for thin markets.

- Polymarket prices-history is the CLOB's sampled price (no bid/ask); thin markets can jump on single trades.

- Series were chosen a priori from the themes in research_notes, not by looking at results; nothing was tuned.

- No transaction costs: the question is only whether any lead exists.
