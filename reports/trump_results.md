# Trump posts vs these stocks

13,731 Truth Social posts 2025-01-20 to 2026-11-18 (trumpstruth.org; text = RSS title, so video/image-only posts carry no text). Prices: yfinance adjusted daily open/close, 2025-01-21 to 2026-11-18.

## Method

- **Timing.** Every return starts at the first daily price printed *after* the post: before 09:30 ET -> that session's open; during the session -> that session's close (13:00 on half-days); after the close, weekends, holidays -> next session's open. Entry mix: 10,452 posts at an open, 3,279 at a close. Nothing is measured from a close before the post, so the immediate intraday reaction is deliberately not counted: this is what a daily-bar trader could actually capture.
- **Events.** Posts on a topic that share an entry point count once (`events`). `event_share` = fraction of all entry points (2 per session) that are events. Baseline = every other entry point, with an open/close dummy so overnight-vs-intraday drift doesn't leak in.
- **Returns** are divided by the stock's 60-day daily vol up to the previous close (no lookahead).
- **Tests** (OLS; z = effect vs 300 placebos that shift the same event dates by a random 20+ sessions, so clustering of posts in volatile weeks and overlapping 5-day windows are kept in the null): `impact` = extra |1-session move| after posts (`abs_move_ratio` 1.2 = moves 20% bigger); `drift_hd` = extra mean h-session return after posts, in vol units; `extra_rev_hd` = extra slope of the h-session return on the move from the prior close to the entry price (negative = moves reverse more than usual after posts, the 'fade the post' trade).
- **Multiple testing:** Holm over all 295 p-values (valid under the heavy dependence between topics and tickers). `passes` lists tests with Holm-adjusted p < 0.05.
- **Data gap:** the feed returns at most 100 posts per request and has no paging; on 11 UTC days with 100+ posts (reposting sprees) only the latest 100 are kept, so a few earlier posts on those days are missing.
- Skipped, no price data: ANT. Pairs with fewer than 10 events, or tickers with under 250 sessions of prices (SPCX, IPO 2026-06: too short for a placebo null), dropped.
