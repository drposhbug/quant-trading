## Data sources and timing

- Form 4s for our issuers: 12103 (2014-01-02 to 2026-03-31); acceptance time found for 100.0%.
- FINRA short interest: 2017-12-29 to 2026-10-30, 12 names.
- TSMC monthly revenue: 2013-10 to 2026-10 (156 months).
- Insider: SEC 'Insider Transactions Data Sets' (quarterly parsed Form 3/4/5, sec.gov/dera) + EDGAR submissions JSON for acceptance timestamps. Info date = acceptance time (ET); after 16:00 or a non-trading day -> next session's close. Officer/director = reporting-owner relationship contains Director or Officer. 10b5-1 = the Form 4 checkbox (from April 2023) or any footnote/remark citing Rule 10b5-1; earlier plans not mentioned in footnotes are misclassified as discretionary. TSM files no Form 4 (foreign private issuer).
- Short interest: FINRA Query API `consolidatedShortInterest` (free, no key). History starts Dec-2017 for these names. Publication date is not in the data; FINRA disseminates ~7 business days after settlement, after the close, so the signal is used at the close of settlement + 8 sessions. Forward return = residual return to the next such close. Dates with fewer than 5 names are dropped. yfinance `Ticker.info` short fields are current-only and not used.
- TSMC: TWSE MOPS monthly revenue tables (mopsov.twse.com.tw t21sc03, consolidated revenue, TWSE code 2330); investor.tsmc.com is behind a bot wall. Release date = the 10th of the following month (the legal deadline; TSMC releases on or before it in Taipei, i.e. before the US open), so the signal is used at the close of the first US session on/after the 10th. If TSMC released earlier, this is late, never early. The same-day reaction on the release day is deliberately not counted (not tradable at close fills).
- One pre-specified form per predictor; no lookback or threshold tuning.
