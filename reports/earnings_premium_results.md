## Events and data

- 729 company ids were S&P 500 members at some point from 2016; 725 mapped to at least one SEC CIK; 28680 Item 2.02 reaction days from 2016 on (724 companies). No CIK (no events, never traded): 2 ids that were members 2017+: SBNY, SOLS.
- CIK mapping: current members from SEC company_tickers.json; every id (and predecessor CIKs) via EDGAR full-text search for '"NYSE: SYM"' / '"Nasdaq: SYM"' in 8-Ks during the symbol's own date spell (a CIK needs >= 25% and >= 5 of the hits). Short or reused tickers can map wrongly or not at all; a wrong CIK adds unrelated filings, which mostly produce expected dates with no announcement (held 10 days, no earnings exposure) and dilute the sleeve toward the basket.
- Expected dates need the prior-year event, so a company's first year in the data, and quarters it moved by more than ~5 sessions, are not traded or are held through no announcement.
- Item 2.02 also covers pre-announcements and some non-earnings releases; 8-K acceptance can lag a press release (reaction day one session late; the trade then exits a day late, which still holds the release).
- Returns: Alpaca adjusted closes (load_universe), clipped at +/-40%; -100% on the session after the last bar for SIVB/SBNY/FRC. Held names without a price that day earn 0. Unadjusted spin-offs of ex-members remain as clipped fake drops.
- Costs: turnover x 5 bp (10 bp stress) on the equal-weight sleeve; daily drift rebalancing and the T-bill leg are free; the mix's rebalancing between model and sleeve is free. Benchmark basket is cost-free.
- One test, pre-registered; the t > 3.0 bar reflects ~700 earlier tests in this project (Harvey-Liu-Zhu).
