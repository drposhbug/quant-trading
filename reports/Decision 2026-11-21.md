## Why no reset

- Every scored statistic already runs in percent from the first logged rebalance on 20 November, so a reset improves
  none of them.
- Alpaca no longer resets paper accounts in place: it means deleting the account, creating a new one and replacing
  the API keys in GitHub. A second manual start is how the first 1.6-point error happened.
- A reset would erase 3.4 points of loss that the model produced as designed, along with 1.6 points of set-up error.
  Real money has no reset, and whether the owner can leave a losing account alone is the scarcest thing the paper
  year measures.
- Standing rule adopted: the paper account is never restarted because of its P&L. The two set-up errors are
  annotated instead, and 20 November is the tracking baseline.

## Amendments from the reviews, adopted

- Measure tracking error on end-of-day equity now (both reviewers). The 15:30 snapshot against a close-to-close model
  breaches the 3% bar from the clock offset alone. Nothing is lost by a short delay: the broker keeps daily closing
  equity. **Not yet done.**
- The aggregate trigger on the no-trade band changes which orders execute, so it waits for the stock-model review.
- Suspend the options iron-fly sleeve until its entry scan is fixed: it cannot trade today (the scan fails on any
  name reporting more than 30 days out), so zero trades is not a test result.
- Retire the Elo sports sleeve from scoring and withdraw the "+6% validated" label in `betting/METHODS.md`.
- The 12 December review is legitimate only with this agenda, fixed now: measurement, the defect list below, the
  pooled favourites test at a venue the owner can trade, and a written specification for the Kalshi sleeve. Stock
  parameters and the no-trade band are out of scope. Until then: no discretionary trades or parameter changes;
  repairs are allowed.
- Promotion bar: no real money on a single PASS; require a second independent sample. Four new tests a year at most.

## How to profit from the selloff

No trade found in this research has positive expected value after costs: not volatility selling, hedges, re-entry
variants, or the Kalshi and Polymarket macro contracts, which match futures and options to within a point. What has
paid individuals in drawdowns needs no forecast: not selling, investing cash already meant for stocks on its normal
schedule, and harvesting real taxable losses before 31 December where they exist (this depends on the owner's own
accounts and lots, which this repo does not know). For the paper model the playbook is to leave it alone. Expect it
to lag a sharp rebound: in past recoveries it made about +6% in the first three months while the fully invested
basket made +15% to +38%, and its volatility scale stays low until late February 2027.
