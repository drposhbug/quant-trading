## Caveats

- Benchmarks: every equal-weight row runs through backtest_returns with the model's 5bp cost. The ex-post vol-matched row scales EW by a constant (0.64) picked with hindsight so its full-sample vol equals the model's: generous to EW. The ex-ante row uses the model's own rule (same vol target, 60d vol, no lookahead) but no regime filter, so its realised vol differs. Sharpe ex T-bill subtracts ^IRX/100/252 daily; idle cash earns 0 in every row (live, idle cash sits in SGOV: universe_results.md C1).
- End-2014 top-10 tech list (universe_study.py TECH10_2014: AAPL MSFT GOOGL META ORCL INTC AMZN CSCO IBM QCOM) is the honest forward expectation: same engine, names picked without knowing the winners.
- Turnover cost is charged on the trade from the drifted previous weights to the new target (so constant-weight EW pays to rebalance its drift), for the model and the benchmarks alike; the first entry from cash is charged.
- Selection bias: these names were chosen knowing they became AI winners (AVGO, AMD, NVDA especially). Absolute returns are inflated; only comparisons on the same names (equal-weight, inv-vol basket, HRP) are meaningful.
- SPCX has ~110 days of history and enters only after 60 days; ANT (Anthropic) has no prices yet and is weight 0.
- Prices are yfinance adjusted closes; fills assumed at the close with flat bp costs.
- DSR/PBO cover the parameter grid only. The shipped config was also chosen over the signal model, HRP, the 8-name universe and a 15% vol target, so the true number of trials is larger than the grid's.
