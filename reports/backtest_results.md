## Caveats

- Selection bias: these names were chosen knowing they became AI winners (AVGO, AMD, NVDA especially). Absolute returns are inflated; only comparisons on the same names (equal-weight, inv-vol basket, HRP) are meaningful.
- SPCX has ~110 days of history and enters only after 60 days; ANT (Anthropic) has no prices yet and is weight 0.
- Prices are yfinance adjusted closes; fills assumed at the close with flat bp costs.
- DSR/PBO cover the parameter grid only. The shipped config was also chosen over the signal model, HRP, the 8-name universe and a 15% vol target, so the true number of trials is larger than the grid's.
