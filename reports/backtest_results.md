## Caveats

- Selection bias: these 8 names were chosen *because* they won. Absolute returns are inflated; only the comparisons against equal-weight and the inv-vol basket (same names, same bias) are meaningful.
- SPCX has ~110 days of history and enters only after 60 days; it barely affects these results.
- Prices are yfinance adjusted closes; fills assumed at the close with flat bp costs.
