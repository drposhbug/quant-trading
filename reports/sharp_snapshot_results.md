# Same-instant Polymarket vs de-vigged Pinnacle: does buying the side Pinnacle favours pay?

## Pre-registration (written 2026-11-20, before any rule result was computed)

**Data.** `price_snapshots` in a copy of `betting/sports_data.sqlite3` (`data/sharp_snap_copy.sqlite3`).
Polymarket and every bookmaker are written with the same `snapshot_time` in one `log_prices.py` run, so the
Pinnacle line is at most the Odds API's own refresh lag old (no hours-old fair value as in the dry trader).

**Fields.** Polymarket `price` = CLOB best ask (cost to buy 1 share now), `bid` = best bid, `ask_size` = shares
at the ask. Rows with no `ask_size` are the Gamma indicative-price fallback, not a live book, and are dropped.
Bookmaker `price` = 1/decimal odds.

**Fair value.** Pinnacle, multiplicative de-vig within one game at one snapshot: p_i = (1/o_i) / sum_j (1/o_j),
2-way (H/A) for NFL/MLB/WNBA/NBA, 3-way (H/D/A) for soccer. Snapshots with an incomplete Pinnacle set are dropped.

**Pre-game only.** A row is used only if snapshot_time < commence_time (Odds API) and < Polymarket's
`gameStartTime` when Gamma reports one.

**Settlement.** Gamma `markets?clob_token_ids=` for every token; a token is settled only if its market is
closed and its outcomePrice is exactly 0 or 1 (voids / 50-50 / open markets are dropped).

**Rule (primary: 3pp; secondary: 5pp).** For each Polymarket token (= game-outcome), walk its pre-game snapshots
in time order and take the FIRST where edge = pinnacle_fair - pm_ask >= 3pp. Buy $1 of that token at
ask + s, hold to settlement. One bet per token. No other filters.

**Costs.** Taker fee per share = r * q * (1 - q) with q = ask + s; r = 0.05 (r = 0.03 for entries before
2026-07-10; also shown with r = 0.03 everywhere). Extra slippage s = 0 / 1 / 2c on top of the ask
(primary s = 0, since `price` is already the ask). $1 stake: P&L = settle / (q + fee) - 1.

**Statistics.** ROI per $ staked; 95% CI from 2,000 bootstrap resamples of whole game days (commence date).
Pass bar (repo standard, MATH.md sports sleeve): CI lower bound > 0 and ROI > 0 in both chronological halves
of the bets, at the primary cost (s = 0, r = 0.05). Reported by league, by halves, and CLV (entry ask vs the
last pre-game Polymarket mid, and how much of the Pinnacle-PM gap closes by then).

**Calibration.** Brier of Pinnacle fair vs Polymarket mid (and ask) against the settled outcome, at the same
instant, by league; game-day bootstrap CI of the paired difference.

**Soft books (informational).** Same rule with DraftKings / FanDuel: first snapshot where
pinnacle_fair - 1/odds_book >= 3pp, $1 at the book's decimal odds, no fee, settled with the Polymarket result.

## Verdict

**Fail, and for a structural reason: at the same instant there is nothing to buy.** On 10,975 clean pre-game
rows (1,299 settled tokens, 550 games, 134 snapshots, Sep 10 - Nov 20 2026) the Polymarket ask was never 3pp or
more below de-vigged Pinnacle. The largest same-instant edge in the whole panel is +2.7pp; the 99th percentile is
+1.3pp; the median is -0.7pp (PM ask sits about a cent above Pinnacle fair, i.e. the PM mid IS Pinnacle fair:
median fair - mid = 0.0c, 5th-95th pct -1.5c to +1.3c). The pre-registered rule therefore placed 0 bets.

**Every as-registered 3pp/5pp signal (10 bets, -48% ROI) was a game-matching error**, not a price: the Polymarket
token belonged to a different game of the same series (WNBA playoff series, MLB series: PM event slug dated 1-3
days away from the Odds API commence_time, identical PM quotes attached to two events) or to the wrong team
(LAFC-Galaxy H token at 0.19 vs Pinnacle 0.65). The integrity filter (token's Gamma `gameStartTime` within 3 h of
commence_time, complete PM outcome set, asks summing >= 0.98) was added after seeing these bets and removes all 10;
both versions fail. **Plumbing bug to fix (not done here, out of scope):** `odds_compare.gamma_find_event` takes the
earliest-ending PM event within 9 days of kickoff, so in a series it can attach the wrong game's token; `log_prices.py`
and `trader.resolve_tokens` both inherit it. Matching on Gamma `gameStartTime` (+/- 3 h) would close it.

**Calibration:** Pinnacle and the Polymarket mid are equally accurate at the same instant (pooled Brier 0.2073 vs
0.2073, paired difference +0.0001 [-0.0004, +0.0006]); no league differs except MLS at the last snapshot
(Pinnacle better by 0.0011, CI excludes 0; 1 of 12 untested-for-multiplicity cells, too small to trade through a
5% fee). Polymarket is a copy of the sharp line, not a lagging one.

**Why the dry trader lost:** with Pinnacle one snapshot stale (median 8 h) the same rule finds 58 "edges"
(0.6% of rows vs 0.0% same-instant) and loses -16% (CI [-54%, +31%]). The edges were the gap between an old line
and a current price, i.e. the market had already moved and Polymarket was right.

**Exploratory (not pre-registered), 1pp threshold:** 94 bets, +16% ROI after fees but CI [-24%, +65%], mostly
longshots (mean ask 0.28), halves +30% / +2%. CLV is barely positive (+0.35c, 54% beat the close) and 73% of the
~1.9c gap closes by kick-off, mostly by PM moving (+0.85c) rather than Pinnacle (-0.49c). That is consistent with
PM tracking Pinnacle with a short lag, but a 1-2c gap is eaten by the 0.05 p(1-p) taker fee (about 1.0-1.25c per
share near 0.3-0.5) and the 1c spread. At 2pp: 12 bets, -65%.

**Soft books (informational):** DraftKings never sat 3pp below Pinnacle fair at the same snapshot (median
-2.25pp, their vig); FanDuel did twice (both lost). Same-instant soft-book lag big enough to beat Pinnacle is
essentially absent at this sampling rate, and any winner would be limited quickly (and is legal only where each
book is licensed).

**Is a fresh-odds live version worth paying for? No.** The Odds API paid tiers (as last published: 20K credits
~$30/mo, 100K ~$59/mo, 5M ~$119/mo) buy freshness, but freshness is exactly what removes the edge: at fresh
prices PM and Pinnacle agree to within the fee. 5-minute polling of 5 sports over ~16 h/day is ~29K credits/mo
(~$59 plan); 1-minute is ~144K (~$119 plan). Expected profit on the only populated bucket (>= 1pp, ~40 bets/month
here, ~0.3c net per share after fee, ~1% per $): at $100/bet about $40/month before noise, at a CI that includes
large losses, against a $59-119/month data bill. Sub-minute dislocations that 2-3 snapshots a day cannot see are
the latency game already rejected in "reports/Millisecond lags and latency arbitrage.md" and the minute-level PM lag study (PM follows within 1-2 min, faster actors win). Do not
buy the plan for this strategy; retire Method 1 (sharp-line convergence) or keep it only as a dry CLV logger.

Caveats: 2-3 snapshots/day, so intra-snapshot dislocations are unobserved; Pinnacle quotes come via the Odds API's
own cache and PM books are read seconds-to-minutes after it in the same run (both directions of lag are small next
to the 8 h staleness being tested); NBA has no Pinnacle/PM rows yet and the 4 July World Cup games have no token
ids, so the pre-July-10 0.03 fee case has no settled bets; multiplicative de-vig (the trader uses power de-vig,
not run; it shifts fair toward favourites and away from longshots by about a cent at typical 2-4% overrounds, too little to turn a +2.7pp maximum into a population of 3pp edges).
