## Verdict

**Null result.** Plain Elo does not forecast better than Polymarket's pre-game price, and betting its disagreements does not clear the bar. The market already contains everything a results-only rating knows (and injuries, rest and lineups, which Elo does not). Most of Elo's 'edges' come from its pre-set home advantage (538's 48/100 points) being too large for today's leagues (see Home bias), so it mostly bets the home side. Re-fitting the home advantage on this sample would be tuning on the test set and is not done. Keep the forward ledger running only as a calibration record; do not bet.

Caveats: no historical order book, so entry = hourly mid + fixed cost; the hourly point nearest start-2h can be stale in thin markets; NFL has few games per season; ties and cancelled markets dropped (NBA preseason, summer league, all-star and NFL preseason markets do not match a results row and are excluded).
