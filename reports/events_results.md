## Data limitations

- Earnings dates are 8-K Item 2.02 acceptance times. Filed before 09:30 ET -> reaction that day; otherwise next session. An 8-K filed hours after an earlier press release would shift the reaction one day late (rare for these names: most file within minutes of the release). Pre-announcements and restated results also carry Item 2.02.
- Tesla also files quarterly delivery numbers under Item 2.02; they are dropped (days 1-5 of a quarter). With them left in, PEAD showed 132bp, t = 3.2, Holm p = 0.01: the apparent drift came from delivery days, not earnings. That is a post-hoc observation, not a tested result.
- For reference only (forced past the gate, not adopted): pre-FOMC max-exposure overlay Sharpe 1.317 vs 1.284, P = 0.16; PEAD tilt Sharpe 1.300, P = 0.25. Neither clears P < 0.05.
- TSM excluded as an announcer (6-K filer, no item codes). SPCX contributes one report (IPO 2026-06).
- Pre-holiday = next session more than one business day away, which also catches unscheduled closures (e.g. 2018-12-05, 2025-01-09 national days of mourning).
- FOMC dates hard-coded from federalreserve.gov; unscheduled meetings excluded.
- Selection bias (MATH.md §7) applies: these names are the decade's winners; residuals vs QQQ reduce but do not remove it.
